## Why regex widening alone is insufficient confidence

Changing [a-z_] to a broader identifier character class would recover the current omission with a small patch, but leaves an independent definition population beside the structural helper and preserves text-based false-edge risks. A callee spelling in comments, ordinary strings, non-leading/triple-single docstrings, attribute calls or nested function text can satisfy the existing regex without being the intended caller's direct dependency. Decorated, multiline or async functions and genuine Unicode identifiers are already supported by Formula enumeration but not faithfully represented by the old body/name regexes.

Existing structural layer should be extended rather than paired with a third definition authority. Parse canonical source once per producer invocation, align eligible public callers/callees with the existing helper's eligibility/duplicate/error contract, and collect actual Call nodes. Exact node/edge dictionaries, existing IMPORTS direction, endpoint filtering, deduplication and self-edge exclusion stay intact. AST Name calls avoid comments/strings/attribute-spelling matches; they do not by themselves solve aliases or scope.

Before coding, the amended design must state the bounded lexical contract. A Name callee must denote the relevant module-level Formula, or an explicit same-module import of it, rather than a parameter/local function/ordinary reassignment/unrelated import with the same spelling. Do not resolve arbitrary variable aliases or attribute chains by final-name coincidence. Explicitly decide supported same-module import aliases versus exclusion; no current missing pair requires arbitrary alias following. Do not infer that 'local' means 'unrelated': preserve the two real self-import bindings above. Comprehension targets can shadow a global function only within their actual scope; generator expressions need the corresponding handling rather than an outer-only lookup.

Nested FunctionDef/AsyncFunctionDef, lambda and class bodies should not silently be attributed to the outer Formula as direct calls. Their default/decorator expressions and body execution differ. Either exclude those nested callable bodies with a clearly stated Phase1 limitation, or separately justify a bounded attribution policy and tests; do not broaden into whole-program call graphs. Full dynamic rebinding, monkeypatching, reflective calls and arbitrary alias propagation are outside a minimal direct-source extractor. A syntactic or conservative contract must be labeled honestly and must not be marketed as runtime reachability.
