## E2-03 — MAJOR — the Dean record's `v_F` is the monolayer Dirac velocity, which is inconsistent with its own `c_s` and flips the sign of the paper's headline shift

- **severity**: MAJOR
- **lane**: parameters
- **Gate**: `parameter_provenance`, `physical_bounds`
- **Location**: `src/core/constants.py` `GRAPHENE_PLATFORMS['Dean_bilayer_nozzle']`
  (`v_F: 1.0e6`, `c_s: 4.4e5`); `PARAMETER_PROVENANCE['Dean_bilayer_nozzle.l_ee_nm']`
- **Observed**: `v_F = 1.0e6` m/s with the comment *"bilayer ≈ monolayer"*, alongside
  `c_s = 4.4e5` m/s. The Lean substrate binds the two by
  `DiracFluidWKB.soundSpeed g = g.vF / √2`, but `1.0e6/√2 = 7.07e5 ≠ 4.4e5`, a 61%
  inconsistency inside one platform record.
- **Evidence**: Geurs p.3 defines the bilayer sound speed as the Fermi-liquid value
  `v_FL(n) = ħ√(πn)/(√2 m*)`, equal to 440 km/s at n = 10¹² cm⁻². The √2 there is `√d` for
  d = 2, not a conformal-Dirac ε = 2p relation; Geurs states the bilayer band is quadratic
  with effective mass m\*. The velocity consistent with the measured c_s is
  `√2 c_s = 6.2e5` m/s, matching `DiracFluidWKB.GrapheneWKBParams`'s own docstring

  which **reverses the sign** of the net T_eff shift from +3.7% to −4.3%. The sign of that

- **Expected**: A bilayer record whose `v_F` is consistent with its `c_s`, and an `l_ee`
  reconciled against Geurs' own 100 nm.
- **Fix**: Either set `v_F = 6.2e5` for the bilayer platform and re-derive `l_ee`, or keep
