# D1's headline reuse figure has drifted for the third time, and is wrong at HEAD

## Summary

**1 MAJOR.** D1 states its cross-platform Lean reuse fraction as **"106 of 114 … approximately
93%"** in eleven places, including a section heading. The deterministic script the paper itself
names as the source — `scripts/count_theorem_reuse.py` — reports **107/120 = 89.17%** at HEAD.

The figure has now drifted three times: `109 of 119 / 92%` → `106 of 114 / 93%` → wrong again.
Each repair replaced one literal with another literal. Finding
`review:2026-05-11-1251-bundle-stage13:D1:6.3` recommended binding it to a `counts.tex` macro
and flagged the literal route as drift-prone; the literal route was taken, and it drifted.

⚠️ **This was invisible until 2026-08-15** because the script had been aborting since
`c791c73b` (2026-08-13) on a parser bug — so for two days nobody could re-derive the claim
either way. Parser fixed in `e2698582`; the drift is what the fix revealed.

- **Severity:** major
- **Lane:** `prose`
- **Verify:** `cd "$REPO" && uv run python -c "import re,subprocess; out=subprocess.run(['uv','run','python','scripts/count_theorem_reuse.py'],capture_output=True,text=True).stdout; m=re.search(r'REUSE (\d+)/(\d+) = ([\d.]+)%',out); assert m,'counter emitted no REUSE line'; n,d,p=m.groups(); tex=open('docs/counts.tex').read(); g=lambda k:re.search(r'\\\\newcommand\{\\\\'+k+r'\}\{([^}]*)\}',tex).group(1); assert (g('reuseTransferred'),g('reusePopulation'))==(n,d),'counts.tex disagrees with the counter'; t=re.sub(r'\s+',' ',open('papers/D1/paper_draft.tex').read()); assert t.count(r'\\reuseTransferred')>=4 and t.count(r'\\reusePercent')>=4,'D1 does not consume the macros'; assert not re.search(r'\b(106|114|109|119)\b *(of|/)',t) and '92.98' not in t and not re.search(r'9[23]\\\\%',t),'a hand-typed reuse literal survives'"`
  *What it asserts:* that the counter, `counts.tex` and the draft all agree by CONSTRUCTION — the macros carry the figure, D1 consumes them, and no hand-typed reuse literal survives. Exits 1 at HEAD.
  ⚠️ **Amended 2026-08-15 — the original Verify demanded the anti-pattern this finding's
  own Fix prohibits.** It asserted `f'{n} of {d}' in t`: the draft must STATE the current

  entirely. So the verify would have passed a fourth hand-typed number and **failed the
  actual repair** — which is exactly what it did when the remediation landed. A Verify that
  contradicts its own Fix enforces the defect.
