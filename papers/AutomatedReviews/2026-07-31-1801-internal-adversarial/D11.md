### 1.1 — 🔴 BLOCKER — the second mandatory novelty carve-out attributes a result to Ljungström & Mörtberg that their paper does not contain, and the substitution is exactly the one that makes the carve-out load-bearing

- **Gate:** CitationIntegrity (SourceFidelity, folded in per `READINESS_GATES.md:238`); also NarrativeGrounding (`attribution-claim`)
- **Location:** `papers/D11/paper_draft.tex:534-538` (rendered: `papers/D11/paper_draft.pdf` p. 8, left column); replicated at `papers/D11/prior_art_novelty.md:76-80`, `src/core/citations.py` `LjungstromMortberg2024` (`provides` + `notes`), and `Lit-Search/Phase-6C/primary-sources/LjungstromMortberg2024.abstract.txt` ("Relevance" + "Caveat" blocks)
- **Observed:** the draft reads, verbatim:
  > Second, Cubical Agda has independently formalized synthetic cohomology including cup products, the Gysin sequence and the cohomology ring of $\mathbb{CP}^\infty$~\cite{LjungstromMortberg2024}: that is precisely the substrate from which Chern classes are \emph{defined} in textbook algebraic topology, so our claim is that no assistant defines the classes, not that the surrounding machinery is absent.
- **Evidence:** I fetched `arxiv.org/abs/2401.16336`, then fetched and text-extracted the PDF (40 pages, `pypdf`) and enumerated every occurrence of the token `CP`:
  ```
  'CP 2,G ) ≅ ⎧'   'CP 2, i.e. the'   'CP 2, Z) ⌣'    'CP 2, Z) ≅ Z[x'
  'CP 2, Z) ≅ /le' 'CP 2, Z) ×H 2('   'CP 2, Z) are g' 'CP 2, Z) ande2'
  'CP 2 is concen' 'CP 2 is 1-conn'   'CP 2 (see also' 'CP 2 (see Sect'
  'CP 2 (7) It fo'  + 3× 'CPP 2020/2023, page'   (the conference name)
  ```
  Every one is $\mathbb{CP}^2$. The section headings are **§5.4 "The complex projective plane"**
  ("$H^*(\mathbb{CP}^2,\mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}[x]/(x^3)$ … formalised in full detail by
  Lamiaux et al. [2023]") and **§5.5 "Infinite real projective space"** ("To further showcase the
  usefulness of the Gysin sequence, let us compute $H^*(\mathbb{RP}^\infty,\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z})$").
  The abstract's own list is "the spheres, torus, Klein bottle, real/complex projective
  **planes**, and infinite **real** projective space." The companion the paper's own novelty doc
  names as related (arXiv:2212.04182, *Computing Cohomology Rings in Cubical Agda*) does not have
  it either — its abstract computes "the spheres and the Klein bottle".
- **Why this is a BLOCKER and not a typo.** The sentence's second clause is the whole argument.
  $H^*(\mathbb{CP}^\infty;\mathbb{Z}) = \mathbb{Z}[c_1]$ is where the first Chern class is
  *defined* ($\mathbb{CP}^\infty = BU(1)$). What the cited work actually computes is
  $H^*(\mathbb{RP}^\infty;\mathbb{F}_2) = \mathbb{F}_2[w_1]$ — the classifying-space cohomology
  for **Stiefel–Whitney** classes — and $H^*(\mathbb{CP}^2) = \mathbb{Z}[x]/(x^3)$, a *truncated*
  ring that is not a classifying-space computation at all. So the carve-out simultaneously
  (a) credits the reference with a result it does not have, and (b) rests its "no assistant
  *defines* the classes, the surrounding machinery is present" concession on the one piece of
  machinery that is in fact absent. A referee who works in HoTT will open §5.5 and see
  $\mathbb{RP}^\infty$. The paper's own §8 says "Four carve-outs are mandatory"; this is carve-out
  two of four, in a paper whose central novelty claim is about Chern classes.
- **Expected:** either the reference contains the $\mathbb{CP}^\infty$ ring (it does not), or the
  sentence names what it does contain.
- **Fix:** replace `the cohomology ring of $\mathbb{CP}^\infty$` with `the cohomology rings of the
  real and complex projective planes and of infinite real projective space`, and rewrite the
  following clause so it no longer asserts this is "precisely the substrate from which Chern
  classes are defined" — it is the substrate for Stiefel–Whitney classes. The honest form is that
  Cubical Agda has the graded-ring and Gysin machinery but *not* the classifying-space computation
  Chern classes are defined from, which if anything **strengthens** D11's novelty claim. Then
  propagate the identical correction to the four other locations above — the cache file is the
  worst of them, because it records "cohomology rings including CP-infinity" as a *verification
  record*.
- **Cache:** fresh-fetch (arXiv abs + full PDF text extraction + companion 2212.04182 abs); record
  appended with verdict `match` for the **bibitem** (title, both authors and venue are correct —
  the defect is in the paper's characterization, not the bibliographic entry), with the
  $\mathbb{CP}^\infty$ discrepancy recorded in the record's `detail`.

## QI Candidate

**qi-a-reviewer-assertion-about-a-ledger-must-be-produced-by-reading-the-ledger.** Round 8 asserted
twice that seven supersession records had been filed; zero had. The assertion was not careless about
the *fix* — round 8 verified those fixes correctly and in detail — it was careless about the
*bookkeeping artifact*, which lives in a different file that nobody opened. The cost is concrete:
nine findings stayed open, D11 stayed RED, and `validate.py` fell from 55/55 to 54/55 across seven
bundles for a reason nobody attributed to it. The same shape appears one layer up in this round's
brief, which attributed a 14 → 18 rise to a code change that a one-command experiment shows has zero
effect on this bundle. **Recommendation:** any statement in a Stage-13 report about graph state,
ledger state or metadata state must quote the command that produced it, the way citation records now
quote the request URL alongside the response. "N records are filed" is a measurement, not a
recollection, and it is one line of Python.

**qi-a-length-based-discriminator-cannot-separate-claims-from-noise.** The notebook gate has now been
through three designs — strings-only, strings-plus-scalars, and strings-plus-scalars-plus-uniform-
array-lengths — and each time the discriminator has been a *structural* proxy for "is this a claim":
string versus number, then numpy versus list, now short versus long. Each proxy was defeated within
one round by a mutation the previous round's author would have called obviously material. The pattern
is that the property being approximated — semantic weight — is not recoverable from the payload's
shape. The way out is not a fourth proxy but a tolerance: compare *values* at a precision coarse
enough that library jitter is invisible and physics is not. **Recommendation:** when a gate needs to
ignore noise, express the noise floor as a numeric tolerance, never as a structural exclusion. A
tolerance states its own blind spot in units; an exclusion hides it in a data type.
