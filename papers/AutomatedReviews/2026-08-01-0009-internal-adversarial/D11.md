## QI Candidate

**The ledger schema cannot express provenance for an artifact outside the repository, and Pipeline
Invariant #11 puts a mandatory Stage-13 artifact class exactly there.**

`READINESS_GATES.md:213` requires a `fixed` finding to carry "a SUPERSEDES edge … or a documented
commit reference", and `build_graph.py:1794-1806` enforces "an explicit `fixed`, a substantive
rationale, and SOME provenance anchor (a commit or a date)". Pipeline Invariant #11 makes
`Lit-Search/Phase-X/primary-sources/<bibkey>.{pdf,abstract.txt,json}` mandatory at every Stage 13.
But `Lit-Search/` is a workspace sibling, not part of this git repo — `git log --all -- 'Lit-Search/*'`
is empty. So for any finding whose artifact is a primary-source cache file, the only reachable
anchors are a bare date, or a SHA that provably cannot contain the change. N3 is the first instance:
the record picked a SHA, and it is the SHA the finding exists to say was insufficient.

This is not a one-off. Three of D11's six rounds raised cache-file findings (`1059:1.3`, `1951:1.1`,
`1951:1.2`), and every one of them is unclosable under the current schema without either a
fabricated commit or a date-only anchor that carries no verifiability at all.

> **Proposed rule.** The supersession `_entry_format` gains an `artifact_anchor` field usable in
> place of `commit`: a `{path, sha256}` pair for out-of-repo artifacts, recorded at closure time.
> `build_graph`'s blocking-closure bar accepts `commit` **or** `artifact_anchor` and rejects a
> `commit` that `git show <sha> --stat` shows does not touch the finding's named Location. The
> second half is the one that would have caught both N3 and `2220:4.3`, and it is mechanical: the
> Location field is already a required part of the finding format.

**Second, smaller candidate.** Six guards in this bundle's history and two more this round
(`_MIN_OVERLAP`, `_LEDGER_DANGLING_BASELINE`) shipped with a constant chosen from a *characterisation*
of the data rather than from *the quantity the predicate compares*. The rule round 12 proposed —
"no threshold without (a) a measurement of the exact quantity compared and (b) a test that plants a
known positive and asserts the check fires" — was written into a comment and not into the process.
N1 is what that costs: the comment above the constant lists the six prior instances by name and its
own calibrating example does not exist in the corpus.
