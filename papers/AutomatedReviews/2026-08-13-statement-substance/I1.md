# Statement-substance review — I1, 2026-08-13

**What this is.** The vacuity classifier tested `True` only against the raw type string, so
a `True` conclusion reached through any binder or arrow scored clean. Fixing that surfaced
13 type-`True` declarations the placeholder registry had never seen, and a companion gate
(`existential_witness_disclosure`) surfaced 5 existential statements a trivial witness
satisfies. This document files the subset with **paper reach into I1**.

**Why I1.** I1 and `paper15_methodology` are the only drafts that quote the Aristotle
verification statistics (`\aristotleruns`, `docs/counts.tex`). None of the theorems below is
cited by NAME in any draft — checked across all `papers/*/paper_draft.tex`. Their reach is
through the **count**: I1 presents `ARISTOTLE_THEOREMS` as a corpus of machine-verified
results, and the entries below are registered in it while proving nothing. That is the
attribution, and it is the honest one; inventing a name-citation to route these would be
worse than leaving them unattributed.

**Severity was NOT tuned to fit a ratchet.** `UNATTRIBUTED_OPEN_BLOCKING_CEILING` stands at
52 of 52 with a standing instruction never to raise it. These are filed against I1 because
I1 is where they actually land, which keeps them in the per-bundle leg on the merits. Any
finding here without genuine I1 reach would have been filed elsewhere at its own severity,
not downgraded.

**Machine-enforced tracking already exists** for every item below and does not depend on
this document: `PLACEHOLDER_THEOREMS` carries a per-entry `resolution`, and
`EXISTENTIAL_ESCAPE_CEILING` / `EXISTENTIAL_MISNAMED_CEILING` are down-only. Remediation
removes an entry; nothing can quietly re-add one.

## Findings

### 1 — 🔴 `acoustic_metric_theorem` is registered as Aristotle-verified and proves only inhabitation

- **Severity:** major
- **Lane:** substrate
- **Gate:** `LeanProofSubstance`
- **Location:** `lean/SKEFTHawking/AcousticMetric.lean:230`
- **Observed:** the statement is `∀ (eos) (bg), ∃ (_ : PhononEOM eos bg), True` — that is
  `Nonempty (PhononEOM eos bg)`, and the proof discharges it by constructing one. Its
  docstring promises the covariant wave equation `□_g π = 0`, the metric read off the
  quadratic expansion and matched against `acousticMetric`, and describes itself as *"the
  central result of Structure A"*.
- **Evidence:** registered in `ARISTOTLE_THEOREMS`. Absent from every vacuity registry until
  2026-08-13 because `_thin_type_label` tested `True` only against the raw type string and
  never against the conclusion it computed. `∃ _, True` was invisible to the check whose
  subject is vacuity.
- **Expected:** a theorem carrying the wave equation its docstring states, or a docstring and
  a registration that match what the statement proves.
- **Fix:** strengthen to the equation, or correct the docstring AND remove it from
  `ARISTOTLE_THEOREMS` — the registration is what gives it paper reach.
- **Verify:** `uv run python scripts/validate.py --check vacuous_statement_audit --check existential_witness_disclosure`

### 2 — 🔴 `dispersive_correction_bound` bounds nothing; it is cited as a quantitative bound

- **Severity:** major
- **Lane:** substrate
- **Gate:** `LeanProofSubstance`
- **Location:** `lean/SKEFTHawking/HawkingUniversality.lean`, cited `src/core/formulas.py:130`
- **Observed:** the conclusion is `∃ δ_disp, ∃ C, 0 < C ∧ |δ_disp| ≤ C·D² ∧ δ_disp ≠ 0`,
  where `D = adiabaticityParam`. Both the bounded quantity and the bounding constant are
  existentially bound, so the inequality is satisfiable whenever `D ≠ 0`. Witness: `δ=1`,
  `C=1/D²`.
- **Evidence:** kernel-checked 2026-08-13 — the shape compiles with zero diagnostics and no
  dispersion relation in scope. The theorem never mentions `dispersiveCorrection`, which
  exists as a project definition. It is registered in `ARISTOTLE_THEOREMS` and reached by
  `formula_grounding`, and was in none of `VACUOUS_STATEMENT_BASELINE`,
  `MODELING_ASSUMPTION_THEOREMS` or `PLACEHOLDER_THEOREMS`.
- **Expected:** a bound on the actual dispersive correction, with `C` universally fixed or
  given explicitly.
- **Fix:** re-point the statement at `dispersiveCorrection`. `kappa_scaling_dispersive_quadratic`
  already proves the quadratic scaling correctly and is the model to follow.
- **Verify:** `uv run python scripts/validate.py --check existential_witness_disclosure`

### 3 — 🔴 `dissipative_correction_existence` is a case split satisfiable by any two reals

- **Severity:** major
- **Lane:** substrate
- **Gate:** `LeanProofSubstance`
- **Location:** `lean/SKEFTHawking/HawkingUniversality.lean`
- **Observed:** `∃ δ_diss, (γ₁=0 ∧ γ₂=0 → δ_diss=0) ∧ (0<γ₁ ∨ 0<γ₂ → δ_diss≠0)`. Witness:
  `δ_diss = 0` in the first case, `1` in the second. Never tied to the dissipative correction.
- **Evidence:** registered in `ARISTOTLE_THEOREMS`. Same module and same authoring session as
  finding 2 — this is a pattern, not an isolated slip.
- **Expected:** a statement about the defined dissipative correction.
- **Fix:** state it against the project definition, as findings 2 and 3 share a repair.
- **Verify:** `uv run python scripts/validate.py --check existential_witness_disclosure`

### 4 — 🔴 Three Aristotle-registered theorems contain no project symbol at all

- **Severity:** major
- **Lane:** substrate
- **Gate:** `LeanProofSubstance`
- **Location:** `OnsagerAlgebra.lean`, `GoltermanShamir.lean` (×2)
- **Observed:**
  - `onsager_infinite_dimensional : ∀ n, ∃ m, m > n` — the Archimedean property of ℕ, named
    for an infinite-dimensionality result. No project symbol appears.
  - `tpf_violates_C2 : ∀ (tpf) (c), ∃ N, N > c.local_dim` — the `tpf` argument appears
    **nowhere** in the statement. The theorem claims TPF violates C2; TPF is not in the claim.
  - `tpf_outside_gs_scope` — same decorative-`tpf` shape.
- **Evidence:** all three in `ARISTOTLE_THEOREMS`. A corpus-wide scan found 216 declarations
  with at least one explicit binder absent from the rest of the type; these three are the
  subset that is both Aristotle-registered and existential.
- **Expected:** statements that mention the objects they are named for.
- **Fix:** state the TPF results against `TPFConstruction`; restate the Onsager result about
  the algebra's dimension.
- **Verify:** `uv run python scripts/validate.py --check existential_witness_disclosure`

### 5 — 🟡 Four "uniqueness" theorems prove existence

- **Severity:** minor
- **Lane:** substrate
- **Gate:** `LeanProofSubstance`
- **Location:** `SKDoubling.lean`, `SecondOrderSK.lean` (×3)
- **Observed:** `firstOrder_uniqueness`, `secondOrder_uniqueness`, `fullSecondOrder_uniqueness`
  and `relaxed_uniqueness_test` all conclude `∃ coeffs, …`. Uniqueness requires `∃!` or
  `∀ c₁ c₂, … → c₁ = c₂`.
- **Evidence:** the statements are otherwise sound — the bound variable is pinned to project
  content — so this is a naming defect, not a vacuity. Registered `misnamed`.
- **Expected:** names matching what is proved, or statements matching the names.
- **Fix:** rename to `_existence`, or strengthen to `∃!`. Prefer strengthening: the KMS
  hypotheses are present and uniqueness is the claim the SK construction actually wants.
- **Verify:** `uv run python scripts/validate.py --check existential_witness_disclosure`

### 6 — 🟡 `WeylDoubletData` and `SMGPhaseData` declare their physics invariants as `: True`

- **Severity:** minor
- **Lane:** substrate
- **Gate:** `LeanProofSubstance`
- **Location:** `GTWeylDoublet.lean:88-94`, `GaugingStep.lean:153-157`
- **Observed:** `has_gap : True  -- Δ > 0`, `chiral_symmetric : True`, `no_goldstones : True`,
  `q_V_commutes : True`, `q_A_commutes : True`, `charges_noncommuting : True`. The structures
  impose no constraint, so every theorem quantified over them holds its hypotheses for free
  and its signature reads stronger than its claim.
- **Evidence:** `WeylDoubletData` simultaneously asserts that the charges commute and that
  they do not. As `True` fields all three are satisfiable at once, which is why the
  inconsistency never surfaced. Registered in `PLACEHOLDER_THEOREMS` 2026-08-13 with
  per-field resolutions; no draft cites them by name.
- **Expected:** fields carrying the conditions their comments name.
- **Fix:** per-field, as recorded in each registry entry's `resolution`.
- **Verify:** `uv run python scripts/validate.py --check vacuous_statement_audit`
