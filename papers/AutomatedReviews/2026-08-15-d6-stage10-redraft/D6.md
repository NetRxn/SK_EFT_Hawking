## D6-S10-01 — Hashing bound is the wrong formula, and the error flips the verdict

- **severity:** BLOCKER
- **lane:** lean
- **Gate:** NarrativeGrounding, ClaimsBacking
- **Location:** `lean/SKEFTHawking/FaultTolerance/APMLdpcHashingBound.lean:237-239`
  (`IsHashingBoundAchievableAt`), `:156-168`
  (`apmLdpc_quEraHarvard_approaches_hashing_bound`); old draft §4 Eq. (hashing_bound).
- **Observed:** the module defines the depolarizing-channel hashing bound as
  `C_hash(p) = 1 - H₂(p)`, and the draft prose asserts that `0.53 ≈ C_hash(0.10)`
  exceeds the code rate `580/1152 ≈ 0.5035`, concluding achievability.
- **Evidence:** Komoto–Kasai arXiv:2412.21171v4 §5.4 Eq. (32), p. 14, verbatim:
  *"Hashing bound for the depolarizing channel with error probability p_D is given as
  follows: R = 1 − H₂(p_D) − p_D log₂(3)"*. §2 p. 3 confirms the channel: *"we consider a
  depolarizing channel where X, Y, and Z errors occur with equal probability"*. The
  omitted `− p log₂ 3` term is the quaternary correction for the Pauli error
  distribution `{1−p, p/3, p/3, p/3}`.
- **Expected:** at `p = 0.10` the source's own formula gives
  `1 − 0.4689956 − 0.1584963 = 0.3725082`, which is **below** the code rate `0.5034722`.
  The correct verdict is **not achievable**; the module and draft conclude the opposite.
  The crossing for a rate-0.5035 code is at `p ≈ 0.0738`, not `0.10`.
- **Fix:** redefine `H` as the quaternary entropy of the depolarizing Pauli distribution,
  or rename `IsHashingBoundAchievableAt` to name the channel it actually describes
  (independent X/Z or dephasing, where `1 − H₂(p)` is correct) and drop every claim
  attributing it to Komoto–Kasai's depolarizing analysis. `H_2_one_half : H_2 (1/2) = 1`
  is also mislabelled "maximum-entropy noise": for a depolarizing channel the
  maximum-entropy point is `p = 3/4`, not `1/2`.
- **Verify:** `! grep -q '1 - H_2 p' lean/SKEFTHawking/FaultTolerance/APMLdpcHashingBound.lean`

## D6-S10-03 — The Williamson–Yoder overhead function and its baseline are both inventions

- **severity:** BLOCKER
- **lane:** lean
- **Gate:** NarrativeGrounding, CitationFidelity
- **Location:** `lean/SKEFTHawking/FaultTolerance/GaugingQEC.lean:70-77` (both defs),
  `:22-35` (the Θ(W²) attribution), `:99-104` (the headline theorem).
- **Observed:** `williamsonYoderAuxQubits W := W * (Nat.log 2 W + 1)` is presented as
  "the Williamson-Yoder overhead function", `naiveQuadraticAuxQubits W := W * W` as
  "the prior-schemes baseline", and prior schemes are said to "generically scale as
  Θ(W²)".
- **Evidence:** Williamson–Yoder arXiv:2410.02213v2. Results, p. 2: *"We prove that the
  worst-case qubit overhead of the gauging measurement procedure applied to an arbitrary
  Pauli operator of weight W is O(W log³ W)"* — one log, cubed, not `⌊log₂W⌋+1`.
  Introduction, p. 1, on prior work: *"The auxiliary system itself incurs a qubit overhead
  of Θ(Wd) where W is the weight of the logical being measured and d is the code
  distance."* Their only quadratic is `Ω(n²)`, and only for good codes where
  `d = Θ(n) = Θ(W)`. No closed-form overhead expression appears in the paper; the
  overhead is characterized as "equal to the number of edges in the graph G" (p. 3).
- **Expected:** the encoded function is *strictly stronger* than what W&Y prove
  (`W log W` versus `W log³ W`), so it is not their result; and the baseline drops the
  distance dependence, so the contrast theorems compare two functions neither of which is
  in the source.
- **Fix:** either encode `O(W log³ W)` against a `Θ(Wd)` baseline with `d` an explicit
  parameter, or remove the attribution and present the functions as the toy comparison
  they are. Note additionally that
  `gaugingQEC_auxQubit_overhead_le : IsLinearPolylogOverhead williamsonYoderAuxQubits`
  unfolds to `f W ≤ 1 * f W` and is discharged by `simp`: the function was *defined* to be
  the bound, so the "headline overhead bound" carries no content even about the toy
  function. See D6-S10-07.
- **Verify:** `! grep -q 'def williamsonYoderAuxQubits (W : ℕ) : ℕ := W \* (Nat.log 2 W + 1)' lean/SKEFTHawking/FaultTolerance/GaugingQEC.lean`

## D6-S10-08 — Three AGP citation errors in the threshold chain's own counting module

- **Gate:** CitationFidelity

  `lean/SKEFTHawking/FaultTolerance/SteaneCode.lean:165`;
  `lean/SKEFTHawking/FaultTolerance/MalignantUnionBound.lean:49`;
  `lean/SKEFTHawking/FaultTolerance/ConcatenatedComposition.lean:37`.
- **Observed:** four modules pin `A_CNOT = 35235` to *"AGP 2006 §8.3, equation (36)"*;

- **Evidence:** AGP arXiv:quant-ph/0504218v3, read at page level.
  1. §8.3 is *"Counting of malignant pairs: Results"* — correct section. But Eq. (36),
     p. 37, states `ε₀ ≥ 2.739 × 10⁻⁵`; it is the **threshold**, not the count. The count
     appears on p. 36 immediately after Eq. (32): *"where A_CNOT = 35,235 malignant
     pairs"*.

     A'_CNOT ≈ 36,511, and ε₀ = (A''_CNOT)⁻¹"*. It is the ancilla-acceptance correction
     `(1 − C/A')⁻⁸` with `C = 50`. The `A → A'` step is a different correction — the cubic
     `B_CNOT` term, their Eq. (19) — worth ≈1.0248.

     `A' ≈ 36,108` and `A'' ≈ 36,511`, giving `ε₀ = 2.739e-5`.
- **Expected:** `1/A_CNOT = 2.838×10⁻⁵` is **not** AGP's rigorous threshold; AGP's is
  `1/A'' = 2.739×10⁻⁵`. The docstrings present the former as the latter.
- **Fix:** correct the equation reference to Eq. (32); correct the 1.0111 operand; delete
  the two fabricated figures. The redraft's §V states the corrected three-stage chain and
  says explicitly which quantity the Lean certifies, so the manuscript no longer depends on
  these docstrings — but the artifact still asserts them. Whether to repin
  `steaneAGPThreshold` to `A''` is a separate design call: the pair-failure recursion the
  Lean actually derives is bounded by `A_CNOT`, and `A'`/`A''` absorb a cubic term and an
  ancilla conditioning that this development does not model.
- **Verify:** `! grep -q '35,625' lean/SKEFTHawking/FaultTolerance/Counting.lean`
