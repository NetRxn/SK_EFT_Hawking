## QI Candidate

**qi-threshold-calibration-consumes-its-own-datum.** Three times in this session a guard's gating
constant was derived from the single most-extreme pair in the live corpus, and the same commit that
set the constant also remediated that pair — leaving the constant above every remaining datum. It
happened at `_PREFIX_FLOOR = 30` (ceiling 18), at `_MIN_OVERLAP = 0.50` (ceiling 0.429), and now at
`_MIN_OVERLAP = 0.40` (ceiling 0.375). The pattern is structural, not careless: a review round
measures the corpus, finds its worst offender, calibrates just under it, and then *fixes the
offender* — so the calibration is invalidated by the round's own success. The general remedy is that
a threshold on a self-remediating corpus must be derived from a **frozen labelled fixture** held in
`tests/`, not from a live sweep; and any check whose threshold exceeds the live maximum must say so
in its summary line rather than reporting "0 contradicted". This affects every guard in
`validate.py` whose constant was measured off the finding corpus, not just this one.
