## QI Candidate

**One systemic pattern, three instances: the project's closure machinery trusts prose written by the party being reviewed.**

1. **A review document can close its own findings.** `build_graph.py:1622-1623` infers `status: fixed` from heading text, so a document authored by the remediator closes findings at any severity with no ledger record, no commit reference and no re-invocation — bypassing Stage 13's "the re-run is evidence" rule and Gate 11's own SUPERSEDES requirement. 66 findings project-wide are closed this way; 9 are at blocking severity and 7 of those are the round-6 reconstruction's. The regex also matches "PARTIALLY FIXED", so two findings the author explicitly marked incomplete are recorded complete. Proposed: `fixed` may come **only** from the ledger; heading inference is restricted to `open`; add a check that no blocking-severity finding is `fixed` without a ledger record.

2. **"Not evaluated" is still spelled "passed" at the top of every readiness guard.** Three rounds have now fixed this one layer at a time — round 6 fixed the P2 hardwiring, round 7 fixed the bare-`except` and the per-bundle no-gate-nodes case, and this round finds the *global* no-gate-nodes case, both `ImportError` handlers, and `ledger_ids_resolve`'s own handler still open. The pattern is that each fix is applied at the site named in the finding and not to the class. Proposed: a one-time sweep asserting that **no** guard in `validate.py` returns `passed=True` from an exception handler or from an empty-input early return, enforced by a test that greps the module's own AST.

3. **Reconstructing a lost round is not the same as knowing how much was lost.** Rounds 4, 5 and 6 of D12 all completed without a review document; only round 6 was reconstructed, and its reconstruction recovers 7 of the round's own 14 findings without saying so. The pipeline has no record of how many Stage-13 rounds a bundle has had, so "was a round lost?" is answerable only by reading commit messages. Proposed: a monotonic per-bundle round counter in `bundle_metadata.json`, incremented by the reviewer, with a check that every round number ≤ the counter has a document on disk minting ≥1 finding for that bundle.
