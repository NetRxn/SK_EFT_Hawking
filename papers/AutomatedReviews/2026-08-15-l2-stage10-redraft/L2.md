# L2 — findings surfaced by the Stage-10 full redraft

## Summary

**2 BLOCKER, 8 REQUIRED, 6 RECOMMENDED.** All were found while drafting, by
reading the two primary sources and the Lean modules directly rather than by
reviewing the prior manuscript. Every prior adversarial review of L2 is void.

The first blocker is a primary-source misreading that inverts the Letter's
second headline claim: the manuscript concludes that modular invariance is a
formal argument for right-handed neutrinos, and **both** cited primary sources
explicitly record the alternative that defeats that inference. It is not a
prose defect alone. It is carried by `bundle_metadata.json.apex_theorems`, by
the charter lede in `docs/PAPER_STRATEGY.md`, and by the naming of the Lean
theorem the claim rests on.

The second blocker is arithmetic. The `lcm(16,3) = 48` "combined without-nu_R
constraint", carried in the manuscript, in `RokhlinBridge.lean`'s docstrings
and in `bundle_metadata.json.notes`, applies the *with*-right-handed-neutrino
modular constraint to the *without* spectrum. Recomputed, both constraints are
`16 | N_f` and the minimum is 16, not 48.

The required findings share one shape, the same one L3's redraft surfaced:
**the formal content is thinner, or lives somewhere else, than the names
suggest.** A theorem named `sixteen_convergence_unconditional` has a
tautological conjunct standing in for one of the four occurrences it
enumerates; a declared apex theorem is half vacuous; the manuscript cited the
arithmetic proxies for the Ext dimensions and never cited the module that
actually computes them; and two theorems named `*_rank_9` and `*_rank_15`
state rank bounds rather than rank equalities.

## 1. BLOCKER

### 1.1 The right-handed-neutrino conclusion is refuted by both cited primary sources

**Severity:** BLOCKER
**Lane:** content
**Where:** `papers/L2/paper_draft.tex` abstract and Sec. "The 16-convergence and the
role of $\nu_R$" and Conclusion (pre-redraft text); `papers/L2/bundle_metadata.json`
`apex_theorems[3]`; `docs/PAPER_STRATEGY.md` "Paper L2" lede.

The manuscript claimed, in three places:

- abstract: "giving a formal independent argument for $\nu_R$ from modular
  invariance alone";
- body: "Since $N_f = 3$ is observed, the joint constraint is direct evidence
  for $\nu_R$";
- conclusion: "therefore $\nu_R$ exists if the modular framing-anomaly
  constraint applies to the SM".

`bundle_metadata.json` records the same claim as an apex:
`central_charge_fractional_without_nu_R` "claims ... a formal argument for
right-handed neutrinos from modular invariance alone".

Both primary sources state the escape route in their own text.

**Wang, arXiv:2312.14928, Sec. IV item 2** ("16 Weyl fermions vs 15 Weyl
fermions?"): for three families of 15 Weyl fermions the reduced conformal field
theory has `c_- = 45/2`, so `c_- = 3/2` is missing, and Wang writes that this
suggests an extra Pfaffian-like non-abelian quantum Hall sector carrying
`c_- = 3/2` in the dimensionally reduced bulk, matching the topological-field-theory
sector of Ultra Unification. Wang's **title** carries the relation
`48/16 = 45/15 = 24/8 = 3`, so the 15-Weyl spectrum is treated as consistent with
three families, not as excluded by them.

**Garcia-Etxebarria and Montero, arXiv:1808.00009, Sec. 4.6** ("Coupling to
TQFTs"), opening sentence: the Dai-Freed constraints "can be altered by adding
Green-Schwarz terms to the action, or more generally by coupling to a suitable
topological field theory, without changing the local degrees of freedom."

So the second constraint the manuscript combines is, by its own source,
alterable by a topological sector; and the first source, when it reaches the
same case, names a concrete such sector.

The Lean theorem does not support the inference either.
`central_charge_fractional_without_nu_R : ¬ ∃ n : ℕ, weyl_central_charge 15 = ↑n`
proves that `15/2` is not a natural number. That is the first horn of a
disjunction, not an existence proof for a particle.

**Fix applied in the redraft:** the section now states the disjunction. An
observed `N_f = 3` is incompatible with a spectrum of exactly 15 Weyl components
per generation *and nothing else*; either right-handed neutrinos are present, or
a compensating sector supplies the deficit without adding local degrees of
freedom, and both named candidates are cited.

**Fix still owed outside the manuscript:**
1. `papers/L2/bundle_metadata.json` `apex_theorems` entry for
   `central_charge_fractional_without_nu_R` must be restated to the disjunction.
2. `docs/PAPER_STRATEGY.md` "Paper L2" lede does not currently carry the claim
   in this form and should be checked when D2 is drafted, since D2 is the deep
   companion that owns the anomaly-constraint argument.
3. Any Lean docstring in `WangBridge.lean` asserting the existence reading.

**Verify:** grep the manuscript and `bundle_metadata.json` for an unqualified
existence claim about right-handed neutrinos. The check must distinguish
"either right-handed neutrinos or a compensating topological sector" (fixed)
from "evidence for", "argument for", "therefore ... exists" (unfixed). A
verify that merely confirms the phrase `\nu_R` appears near `15/2` would pass
while the defect is present, because the defect is the modality of the
conclusion, not its subject.

### 1.2 The `lcm(16,3) = 48` without-$\nu_R$ constraint is arithmetically wrong

**Severity:** BLOCKER
**Lane:** content + substrate
**Where:** `papers/L2/paper_draft.tex` Sec. "The 16-convergence and the role of
$\nu_R$" (pre-redraft); `lean/SKEFTHawking/RokhlinBridge.lean` §5 docstring and
the `constraints_without_nu_R` docstring; `papers/L2/bundle_metadata.json`
`notes` ("lcm(16,3)=48 without-nu_R combined constraint").

The manuscript argued: without right-handed neutrinos the $\mathbb{Z}_{16}$
anomaly requires `16 | N_f` and modular invariance requires `3 | N_f`, so
`N_f` must be a multiple of `lcm(16,3) = 48`.

The second premise is false for the spectrum it is applied to. `3 | N_f` is a
consequence of `c_- = 8 N_f`, and `c_- = 8 N_f` is the *sixteen*-component
spectrum. At fifteen components `c_- = 15 N_f / 2`. Modular invariance then
requires that this be an integer and divisible by 24:

- `N_f` odd: `15 N_f / 2` is not an integer. Excluded outright.
- `N_f = 2m`: the condition is `24 | 15 m`, and since `gcd(15,24) = 3` this is
  `8 | 5 m`, and since 5 is a unit mod 8 it is `8 | m`, i.e. `16 | N_f`.

So the modular condition without $\nu_R$ is `16 | N_f`, the same as the
$\mathbb{Z}_{16}$ condition. The two coincide rather than compound. The minimal
generation count is **16**, not 48. Verified numerically: at `N_f = 16` the
charge is `c_- = 120 = 24 x 5` and the anomaly index `15 x 16 = 240 = 0 mod 16`,
so both hold; the admitted sets `{16, 32, 48, ...}` are identical.

The Lean theorem `constraints_without_nu_R : Nat.lcm 16 3 = 48` is a true
arithmetic identity. The defect is entirely in the physics step attached to it,
which is why a check that the theorem resolves and that `lcm(16,3) = 48` cannot
find this.

A second tell: Wang's title ratio is `48/16 = 45/15 = 24/8 = 3`, where **48 is
the total Weyl count of three sixteen-component families**, not a generation
count. The manuscript's "minimal `N_f = 48`" reads as that 48 relocated into
the wrong slot.

**Fix applied in the redraft:** the section derives the without-$\nu_R$ modular
condition from `c_- = 15 N_f / 2` and states that the two conditions coincide
at `16 | N_f`, each excluding `N_f = 3` on its own. This is strictly stronger
for the Letter's argument, because the disjunction no longer needs the two
constraints combined. `constraints_without_nu_R` is no longer cited.

**Fix still owed outside the manuscript:** the `RokhlinBridge.lean` docstrings
and the `bundle_metadata.json` notes both assert the 48 and will be quoted.

**Verify:** recompute the admitted set of `N_f` under `24 | 15 N_f / 2` and
compare it to `48Z`. The check must compare *sets*, not confirm that
`lcm(16,3) = 48`; the identity is true and passes while the defect is present.

(∑ f : SMFermion, components f) = 16          -- the Weyl count
(16 : ZMod 16) = 0                            -- proved `by decide`
(∀ M : SmoothSpinManifold4, (16 : ℤ) ∣ M.sig) -- the Rokhlin divisor

clean and disclosable design, but the name does not say so.

**Fix applied in the redraft:** the four-occurrence enumeration is demoted from
a section to one sentence in the Discussion, and the sentence says explicitly
that only the Rokhlin occurrence carries a proof here.

**Verify:** the check must assert that no manuscript sentence attributes the

### 2.3 The manuscript cited the arithmetic proxies for the Ext dimensions, not the module that computes them

**Severity:** REQUIRED
**Lane:** content
**Where:** `papers/L2/paper_draft.tex` Sec. "Machine-checked Ext computation" (pre-redraft);
`lean/SKEFTHawking/A1Ext.lean` vs `lean/SKEFTHawking/A1ExtSubstantive.lean`

The manuscript cited `A1Ext.lean` as recording "the resolution-rank values
1, 2, 2, 2, 3, 4". Those theorems are:

```
ext_dim_0 : Fintype.card (Fin 8)  / 8 = 1
ext_dim_4 : Fintype.card (Fin 24) / 8 = 3
ext_dim_5 : Fintype.card (Fin 32) / 8 = 4
```

They are arithmetic identities about the ranks of the free modules. `A1Ext.lean`'s
own successor module says so: `A1ExtSubstantive.lean`'s header calls them
"*arithmetic proxies*".

`A1ExtSubstantive.lean` contains the real computation and the manuscript never
cited it, even though `bundle_metadata.json` names
`SKEFTHawking.A1.ext_dims_substantive` as an apex theorem. It defines the dual
coboundary matrices `delta0..delta4` by augmentation extraction from the
certified differentials, proves each is the zero matrix
(`all_dual_coboundaries_vanish`), and then computes genuine `Module.finrank`
values over `F2` of the resulting kernel and cokernels, giving 1, 2, 2, 2, 3, 4.
`ext4_homology_dim_substantive` does `n = 4` as a literal subquotient
`ker δ⁴ / im δ³`. I verified `ext_dims_substantive`'s axiom set: `propext`,
`Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`. Kernel-pure, no `native_decide`.

The vanishing of the dual coboundaries *is* the content of "a minimal resolution
computes Ext with zero differentials". The manuscript hedged that step as an
unformalized "standard minimal-resolution-implies-Ext-dimension lemma" while the
repository had already discharged it concretely.

A related overstatement survives outside the manuscript. `docs/PAPER_STRATEGY.md`
and `bundle_metadata.json.notes` both call this "the first machine-checked
`Ext^n_{A(1)}(F_2,F_2)` computation". No categorical `Ext` appears in any
statement in any of these modules, and `A1ExtSubstantive.lean`'s own header
defers the categorical identification via Mathlib's `ProjectiveResolution.isoExt`
to future work.

**Fix applied in the redraft:** the section rests on `A1ExtSubstantive.lean`,
states the dual-coboundary vanishing as the substantive step, distinguishes the
kernel-pure `decide` proofs there from the `native_decide` chain-complex
identities in `A1Resolution.lean`, and scopes the claim to the cohomology of the
dualized minimal resolution rather than to the categorical Ext functor.

**Verify:** the check must assert that the manuscript's Ext claim names
`A1ExtSubstantive.lean` and that no sentence claims a formalized categorical
`Ext`. A check that the dimensions 1,2,2,2,3,4 appear and resolve to some Lean
theorem passes while the defect is present, since the weak proxies also carry
those numbers.

### 2.5 The Kitaev / Fidkowski-Kitaev attribution is wrong on three counts

**Severity:** REQUIRED
**Lane:** content
**Where:** `papers/L2/paper_draft.tex` Sec. "The 16-convergence" (pre-redraft);
`lean/SKEFTHawking/RokhlinBridge.lean` header comment

The manuscript wrote: "the period of the Kitaev periodic table of free-fermion
topological superconductors in symmetry class~D, reduced from $\mathbb{Z}$ to
$\mathbb{Z}_{16}$ under interactions". I read Kitaev 2009 Table 1 (p.~2)
directly. It is headed "Classification of **free-fermion** phases", its text
states "The large table shows a **period 8** pattern", and its real-class
column $\pi_0(R_q)$ runs $\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Z}_2, \mathbb{Z}_2, 0, \mathbb{Z},
0, 0, 0$. **There is no sixteen anywhere in the free-fermion table**, and class
D in $d = 2$ is $\mathbb{Z}$.

Fidkowski and Kitaev, for their part, treat a **one-dimensional** Majorana
chain in class BDI with $\hat{T}^2 = 1$, and the interacting collapse is
$\mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}_8$, not $\mathbb{Z}_{16}$.

So the dimension, the symmetry class, the periodicity and the resulting group
are all wrong. The load-bearing error is the phrase **"free-fermion"**: the
$\mathbb{Z}_{16}$ is the *interacting* classification, which both primaries say
explicitly. The sixteen itself is not the error, and must not be
over-corrected: `KitaevSixteenFold.lean` proves
`kitaevCentralCharge (ν + 16) = kitaevCentralCharge ν + 8` plus faithfulness
across the sixteen classes, which is the genuine 16-fold way and makes no
free-fermion claim. Cite it for what it proves or not at all.

This finding reached L2 from a D2 drafter via the wave lead; I verified it
against the table rather than inheriting it.

**Fix applied in the redraft:** the whole four-occurrence enumeration is cut,
and both bibitems with it. The recurrence of 16 is one Discussion sentence
naming only occurrences the Letter can support.

**Verify:** assert no manuscript sentence attributes a $\mathbb{Z}_{16}$
free-fermion classification to either bibkey. Confirming both bibkeys are
registered and cached passes while the defect is present; they are.

### 2.6 The $E_8$ manifold is spin, and the manuscript said it is not

**Severity:** REQUIRED
**Lane:** content + substrate
**Where:** `papers/L2/paper_draft.tex` Sec. "The 16-convergence" (pre-redraft),
"the $E_8$ manifold has $\sigma = 8$ but is not spin";
`lean/SKEFTHawking/RokhlinBridge.lean` docstring for `hirzebruch_weaker`,
"it is NOT spin (it has a non-trivial Stiefel-Whitney class $w_2$)"

The $E_8$ form is even, so by Wu's formula $w_2 = 0$ and Freedman's $E_8$
manifold **is** spin. What it is not is smoothable, and Rokhlin's theorem is
precisely why: a smooth structure would force $16 \mid 8$. Stating it as
"not spin" removes the counterexample's whole point.

The same docstring additionally attributes $8 \mid \sigma$ to the Hirzebruch
signature theorem, which gives no such divisibility ($\mathbb{CP}^2$ has
$\sigma = 1$). The 8 comes from evenness plus unimodularity, which is what
`eight_dvd_latticeSig` proves.

**Fix applied in the redraft:** the sharpness sentence compares against "the
eightfold divisibility an even unimodular form already carries" and states that
a closed topological 4-manifold carrying the $E_8$ form admits no smooth
structure.

**Verify:** assert the manuscript does not call the $E_8$ manifold non-spin and
does not attribute $8 \mid \sigma$ to Hirzebruch.
