  and obtained `r* = 1.1913538`; the pinned `1.19135` gives `0.29999643`.

### 2.2 — 🔴 BLOCKER — ✅ FIXED — three artifacts, one factor, two incompatible explanations

- **Gate:** ComputationCorrectness / CrossPaperConsistency
- **Location:** `papers/D12/paper_draft.tex`; `src/core/formulas.py` (`phonon_psd_gradient_factor`);
  `lean/SKEFTHawking/Electrothermal/BolometricFloors.lean` (`phononPSDGamma`)
