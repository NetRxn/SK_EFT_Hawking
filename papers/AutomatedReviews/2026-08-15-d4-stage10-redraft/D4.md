# D4 — findings surfaced by the Stage-10 full redraft

## Summary

**3 BLOCKER, 6 REQUIRED, 5 RECOMMENDED.** Every one was found by reading
elaborated Lean statements out of `lean/lean_deps.json` and by reading primary
sources at page level, not by reviewing the prior manuscript. The redrafted
manuscript states only what the substrate supports and routes around each of
these, so none is a live defect *in the new draft*; they are defects in the
substrate, the apex registry, the citation cache and a sibling bundle that the
redraft had to work around.

The three blockers share one shape and it is the shape the portfolio keeps
producing: **a declaration's name carries a claim its statement does not.**
D4 is the worst instance measured so far. Its "central QEC biconditional" is
the positivity criterion for the natural logarithm; its "Hopf-link matches
$S$-matrix" theorem contains no $S$-matrix; and seventeen declarations
reachable from D4's sections reduce to `True`, to $a\cdot a = a^2$, to
$6^2 = 36$, to `Iso.refl`, or to an implication from a reflexivity hypothesis
to itself.

The required findings are citation-integrity and arithmetic: an exponent
attributed to a paper whose held abstract states a different one, a numbered
lemma attributed to a paper held only as an abstract, a constant chain whose
reported value cannot be reproduced, an arithmetically false quantum dimension
contradicted by the bundle's own theorems, and a pentagon equation proved for
a different matrix than the one the headline theorem uses.

**Mechanical state after the redraft.** The manuscript compiles at 29 pages
with zero errors and zero unresolved references or citations. Gates requiring
a built `.lake` were not scored, per the standing rule that a fresh worktree
reds them out for reasons unrelated to the work; nothing in that family is
