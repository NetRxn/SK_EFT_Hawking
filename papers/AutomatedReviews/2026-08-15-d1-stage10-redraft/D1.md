# D1 — findings surfaced by the Stage-10 full redraft

## Summary

**2 BLOCKER, 6 REQUIRED, 3 RECOMMENDED.** All were found while drafting, by
reading the Lean *statements* and the numerical pipeline rather than by
reviewing the prior manuscript. The redraft states only what the substrate
supports, so none of these is a live defect *in the new draft*; they are
defects in the substrate, the generator script, the bundle metadata and the
charter that the new draft had to route around.

The two blockers are arithmetic. One is a factor of two in the crossover
frequency that has been carried in the abstract, the body, the generator
script and the charter simultaneously, in three mutually inconsistent
resolutions. The other is a `claims` string asserting a cross-derivation
agreement that the theorem it names does not state and that no declaration in
the module could state, because the second derivation has no formal
representative anywhere in the tree.

The required findings share one shape: **the formal content is thinner than
the identifier suggests, and the `claims` metadata asserts the thicker
reading.** Three theorems in the D1 apex list are content-free or vacuous
against their advertised claims; one headline equation of the polariton
section has no Lean representative at all; and the second-order parameter
reduction is attributed to a theorem that does not state it.
