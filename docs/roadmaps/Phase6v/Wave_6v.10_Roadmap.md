## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **10.A — Reconciled evidence and frozen claim.** Pin the accepted manual source and reviews for both bosonic models and any graphene comparator, their original counterexamples, the current Lean declarations, the public source versions, and the affected producers and consumers. Record the observable, state, generator, norm, reference units, physical regulator, and numerical cutoff. Compare the candidate spatial and receiver routes against the actual DKM hypotheses, including the role of the thermal state. Select a carrier with an explicit test for physical transfer, or substantiate the obstruction for the claimed domain. Record the disposition of each route before proof dispatch.
- [ ] **10.B — Polariton growth.** Derive the actual all-order growth sequence/bound or obstruction for the specified observable and full generator. Account for the drive and loss terms, local density or current, units, common domains, and finite-to-larger-carrier error. Distinguish a global norm, a state seminorm, and a norm on any justified quotient. Show how constants depend on the physical regulator and numerical representation. Include zero drive, absent modal overlap, and high-frequency or high-energy countercontrols. A receiver smoothing bound carries the intrinsic claim only with a proved correlator transfer, with finite observation-window effects included.
- [ ] **10.C — BEC growth.** Derive the result from the specified spatial Hamiltonian, observable, state, and boundary/domain choices. Preserve the reviewed constant-observable conservation case and divergent higher-moment controls. Quantify the energy, volume and regulator limits used by the proposed claim and the microscopic-to-effective reduction where used. A result may be an upper bound, a genuine lower bound, or a domain obstruction. A continuum claim requires uniform control or an explicit obstruction. The prescribed factorial witness supplies a comparison once the physical sequence is derived.
- [ ] **10.D — Transport and public-data applicability.** Use held and public sources to bind the physical regime, coefficients, normalization, and errors. Compute or bound the actual density/current correlator and transport tuple for any retained MIR claim. State the DKM applicability and uniqueness hypotheses separately from F3, and show where they hold or fail. For a physical comparison, establish a common declared inference domain and conclusions that survive the error budget. If the data are insufficient, identify a specific input and precision that would change the result and quantify what can still be stated. Include the interacting graphene comparator only for claims that require it.
- [ ] **10.E — Independent, native and numerical acceptance.** Obtain independent review of each substantive result. Formalize the accepted bridge with existing DKM predicates where they match the statement, preserving kernel purity and checking imports, exact-source dependencies and canonical extraction. Validate the actual computational path against an independent reference with limiting cases, cutoff/error controls and falsifiers. Put formulas, parameter provenance and citations in the canonical registries. Use Stage 11 computational notebooks if needed for reproducible results and the repository's ordinary gates for the changed surfaces.
- [ ] **10.E — Canonical propagation and disposition.** Reconcile the reviewed source correction with the current Python helper, Lean descriptions, provenance, claims and bundle consumers. Verify corrected producer and consumer behavior on the exact candidate, then use the single canonical finding writer for supported closures. Update the owning roadmaps with independent evidence, remaining domain limits, and any outstanding external requirement. Use the [wave pipeline](../../WAVE_EXECUTION_PIPELINE.md) for document sync and fresh adversarial review. Human publication approval remains a separate step.
