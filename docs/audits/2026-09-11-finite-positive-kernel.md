## Mathematical result

`SKEFTHawking/FinitePositiveKernel.lean` constructs a complex coefficient seminorm
from a finite positive-semidefinite matrix, identifies its null space, and uses
the separation quotient to obtain a finite-dimensional complete inner-product
space. Kernel entries are recovered as inner products of spanning feature vectors.
The null relation comes from the kernel; positive definiteness is not assumed.

The all-ones two-point kernel is nonzero and degenerate: a nonzero coefficient
vector becomes zero in the quotient while a feature remains nonzero. A Hermitian
matrix with positive diagonal but a negative quadratic value demonstrates that
entrywise or diagonal positivity is insufficient.

The reflection interface uses an involution and an injective positive-sector
embedding, with kernel entries evaluated between reflected and positive points.
The example has a nonidentity reflection and disjoint sectors. Hermitian symmetry
and full complex quadratic positivity are explicit hypotheses. This is a finite
reconstruction example; no continuum OS/Wightman reconstruction is claimed.

The quantum consumer constructs probe Gram matrices from the actual positive
output of an existing instrument branch. Impossible branches and singular states
are permitted without conditional normalization. No general channel contraction
or faithful logarithm follows from that construction.
