## Findings

**1. Finite-rank flat extension cannot close full R3.** R3's Dcap prescribes the empty-word pairing for arbitrary original compact-polynomial caps with independent dual tests. Fix one original SU(2) link and let t be its real U11 coordinate. Use input caps tʲ times the exterior unit and dual caps tⁱ times the oriented exterior top. For i,j = 0,…,N−1, the pairing matrix is the Haar moment matrix ∫tⁱ⁺ʲ dμ. It has rank N because Haar has full support and a nonzero polynomial in t cannot vanish on its interval of values. Any d-dimensional realization of the cap vectors has cross-pairing rank at most d. This rules out a finite-dimensional representation of all those caps at the base source. It leaves finite cyclic or sewn subspaces and infinite-dimensional W1 open. Popovych’s theorem can still diagnose selected finite blocks. It supplies neither the extra quotient relations nor the external cap-vector representation. The more general flat-extension theorem and pinned Mathlib GNS do not supply those missing inputs. [Original cap nondegeneracy](ORIGINAL_COMPLEMENT_CONNECTOR_MATH_REVIEW_R1.md#L17-L25) · [Popovych, §4](https://arxiv.org/html/0904.0971v2#S4) · [Mourrain–Schmüdgen](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.4975v1)

**2. The dressed shear and literal temporal source share closed plaquette order but differ as source polynomials.** PR115's positive scalar test used a four-site pure-g2 calculation with a traceless spatial coframe shear and fixed links and measure. R3 uses the fixed 16-site nonzero-coupling germ, the original local temporal J00 and an independent time-space frame variation. At identity links the PR115 fixed-link shear's first gravity-action vertex is zero: its determinant term is invariant and the curvature trace vanishes. R3's Q00 has coefficient 1/2 on the ordered word b1 b2 a1 c2 at the same link value. No scalar rescaling identifies those polynomials pointwise. This witness does not settle an integrated correspondence or a derived simultaneous deformation of source, links, maps and measure. Both source and matter calibration still need to be derived on the same regulator. [Branch selection and source variants](https://github.com/NetRxn/SK_EFT_Hawking/blob/c0a775c942fc62528fec2d0b645e4dfe858ae61c/docs/audits/2026-11-05-l1-physical-bridges/PHYSICAL_BRANCH_SELECTION.md#L37-L76) · [Exact Q00 control](ORIGINAL_TEMPORAL_Q00_SOURCE_LIFT_SOURCE_REVIEW_R1.md#L13-L21)

**3. Existing positivity theorems have conditional uses.** The geometric EHC reflection defect is nonzero at the adopted base, so the existing geometric reflection does not meet the reflection invariance required for direct use of the Jaffe–Janssens criterion. The free rational Dirac pole/residue theorem needs a derived covariance in that class. Certified spectral bounds are a later W4 tool once a positive spectral representation for the exact same observable is established. There is no accepted transfer of these results to the original signed, interacting compact source. [Jaffe–Janssens, Thm. V.10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1607.07126v1#page=28) · [Handrack–Salmhofer, Thm. 1.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10685v1#S1) · [Mutzel–Tilloy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09791v1)
