## Primary facts

### Höhn: an actual conditional norm construction

[1401.6062v3](https://arxiv.org/html/1401.6062v3), sections3.2,4,4.4,
Lemmas4.1-4.3, AppendixA: ordinary real configuration spaces, a positive
kinematic L2 representation, self-adjoint constraints and a specified
momentum-linear/global-gauge class yield a reduced L2 physical form.
The general constrained discussion assumes anomaly-free first-class algebras,
continuous spectra and suitable gauge orbits. The propagator measure carries
quantum information; reverse/inverse kernel relations are conditions, not
consequences of writing an action exponential. This source provides a useful
conditional target, not a graded compact-Haar/Berezin prescription for our
action. Lead checked the phase-conjugation and Faddeev-Popov steps in AppendixA.

### Dittrich-Höhn: discrete classical constraint relations

[1303.4294v3](https://arxiv.org/html/1303.4294v3), sections3.1-3.3,
Theorem3.1,5.3: pre/post Legendre maps and constrained presymplectic evolution
are derived for ordinary smooth discrete variational systems. Locally constant
Hessian/two-form rank is retained. This supplies a classical template; a graded
version and actual rank hypotheses must be established here. It supplies no
physical quantum norm.

### Giulini-Marolf: conditional rigging-map uniqueness

[gr-qc/9902045v2](https://arxiv.org/html/gr-qc/9902045v2), sectionsII,III.2:
a unitary constraint-group representation, suitable invariant L1 testspace and
nonzero group-average form underlie uniqueness up to scale. The authors
explicitly leave general group-average positivity unsettled. Auxiliary
representation/adjoint choices precede the theorem. It does not generate our
unknown additional constraints or repair them by averaging the already solved
compact Gauss symmetry again.

### Mišković-Zanelli: regularity must be checked at the right object

[hep-th/0302033v2](https://arxiv.org/html/hep-th/0302033v2), section2 and
section6 equation47: constraint Jacobian independence, degeneracy and
higher-order zero-set replacement have distinct dynamical implications.
Ordinary higher-power constraints can obstruct naive delta-function
quantization. These results do not automatically classify Grassmann-nilpotent
constraints or give a positive form. Equal ordinary zero sets alone do not
justify replacing the original constraint relations.
