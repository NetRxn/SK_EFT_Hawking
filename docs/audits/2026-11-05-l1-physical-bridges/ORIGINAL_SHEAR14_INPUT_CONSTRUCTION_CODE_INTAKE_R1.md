## Static bounds and guard meanings

For a straightforward no-tree factorization of one fully specified term: 64 frame occurrences (56 S4, 4 EHC, 4 q²), 14 epsilon factors, 2 curvature factors. With four plaquette link occurrences per curvature there are 72 compact-coordinate occurrences. If each occupied anchor of degree d receives the existing moment chain of d transition factors plus one terminal factor, there are at most 72+64 moment factors, hence at most 216 factors total. This is a structural upper bound for that representation, not an observed count and not a proof of adequate memory; some anchors or zero factors reduce it. If other factorization/aggregation is adopted it must be recomputed.

A curvature_table with four free coordinate legs and ordered internal pair a != b has at most 8^4*12=49152 outer keys, each containing one zero-mask row; fixed legs reduce that bound. An epsilon factor has 24 rows. A frame table has at most 8*4 outer keys and each frame's two 4x4 bilinear pieces have at most 32 mask contributions, giving a conservative 1024 slots before cancellation; verify representation assumptions after adaptation. Moment-chain row count depends on degree and reachable count states; no useful universal below-cap bound was established here. Factor row and pair counts for actual N16 joins are unknown.
