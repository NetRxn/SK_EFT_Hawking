The alternating projection consequently preserves H1; smooth approximation followed by that projection makes H1_a dense in H_a. For the total density defined with the factor N, permutation symmetry makes all spin-summed marginals equal. Tonelli gives integral rho=N for normalized states. ## Equal densities really produce a common ground state (§3)

Writing D=integral(v-w)rho, the two ordinary variational inequalities are E_v<=E_w+D and E_w<=E_v-D. Both nonnegative gaps sum to zero, so both inequalities are equalities. Each chosen state therefore attains the other form's infimum. This works for arbitrary selected ground vectors in degenerate ground spaces; it does not infer that a ground vector or density is unique.

Choose either common normalized state. Subtracting its two weak equations cancels the identical kinetic and interaction forms on the same carrier. The residual h=[sum_i(v-w)(xi)-(E_v-E_w)]Psi is in L2 by Hardy. Its multiplier is symmetric under every particle permutation, so h itself lies in H_a. Orthogonality to the dense H1_a test subspace therefore implies h=0 in H_a and hence in the full ambient L2 space. Without the permutation-symmetry observation only a projected equation would follow; the note explicitly supplies the needed observation. The resulting equality holds a.e. in every spin component because there are finitely many components.

## The analytic zero-set lemma is proved, not assumed (§4)

For real analytic f on connected open U, the set A where every partial derivative, including f, vanishes is relatively closed by continuity. It is relatively open: at such a point the convergent Taylor series vanishes in a neighborhood, making all derivatives vanish there. Thus if f is not identically zero, connectedness forces A empty.

At a zero p of a nonzero f, some finite-order derivative is nonzero. The corresponding directional kth derivative is a nonzero homogeneous polynomial in the direction; some real direction therefore makes it nonzero at p. Continuity gives a small rotated rectangular box where that directional derivative never vanishes. On every parallel line section in this box, k+1 distinct zeros would contradict repeated Rolle's theorem. Each line section of the zero set is therefore finite and has one-dimensional measure zero. The zero set is measurable since f is continuous; Fubini and rotation invariance give measure zero in the box. Nonzero points have zero-free neighborhoods. A countable subcover exists by second countability, so the entire zero set is null. This contradicts a positive-measure zero set and proves the lemma.

No minimum order uniform over U is assumed, and the local directional boxes need not have a common orientation. Coulomb 1/|x-A| is real analytic off its center by the local binomial series in the note. Finite sums and coordinate pullbacks retain that property. The lemma is applied to the external-difference multiplier, never to wavefunction regularity or its nodes.

## Positive-measure support and the Coulomb conclusion (§5)

Remove the finite union B of nuclear centers. Omega=R3 minus B is open and path connected; polygonal paths can detour around finitely many points. Its N-fold product is connected and open. Its configuration-space complement is null: each excluded coordinate-center cylinder is null, including on unbounded space by a countable bounded-box exhaustion. Electron-electron collision sets need not be removed because the identical interaction has already cancelled.

A normalized finite-spin state has at least one component of positive L2 norm. Its nonzero set has positive Lebesgue measure. Removing the exceptional null sets from the L2 identity and from Omega^N preserves a positive-measure set where the analytic multiplier f vanishes. It follows that f vanishes everywhere on Omega^N. This uses no division by Psi and no assumption that Psi is nonzero a.e.; nodal sets may be large.

Fixing the other coordinates and varying x1 shows v-w is constant on Omega. For N=1 there are no fixed coordinates and the same conclusion follows directly. Evaluating f at a tuple then gives E_v-E_w=N times this constant. Taking x to infinity makes every finite Coulomb term vanish, identifying the constant as c_v-c_w. After coincident centers are aggregated, multiplying the remaining identity by |x-A| and taking x to each distinct center isolates its coefficient. Every other term stays bounded near that center. All residual coefficients vanish. This proves equality of the weighted atomic measures, not equality of an arbitrary ordering, multiplicity or list of zero-charge labels. Signed coefficients and cancellation are handled by the same finite-sum argument.

Conversely v->v+c adds Nc||eta||² to the form. It shifts the infimum by Nc and preserves every ground-state set and its set of total densities. Empty ground-state sets are not asserted nonempty by this observation.
