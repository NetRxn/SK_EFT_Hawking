## Acceptance statement

Use the actual Torus, Links, linkHaar, Gaussian coordinates Auxiliary L, and actual printed and closed source polynomials from the finite Eq10 theorem. Let T_a(U) be the existing local coframe embedded in Grassmann L, let N = 4 * volume L, and write E_S for the exact nilExp of the sum of evaluated site sources. For arbitrary J : Auxiliary L → ℂ let E_J = nilExp N (Σ_a J_a • T_a). Define N_0, N_a, N_ab as the Haar integral of Berezin applied to E_S E_J, T_a E_S E_J, and T_a T_b E_S E_J. Define W exactly as the original fullWeight for S, independent of J. For σ > 0 and v = σ², with joint μ = linkHaar × gaussianMeasure(v), prove

- N_0 = ∫ W(h,U) D_J(h,U) dμ.
- N_a = ∫ (q h_a W − ∂a W) D_J dμ.
- N_ab = ∫ [(q² h_a h_b − q δ_ab)W − q h_a ∂b W − q h_b ∂a W + ∂b∂a W] D_J dμ.

Here q = ((σ : ℂ)²)⁻¹, D_J = det(globalMatrix L U (fun a => (h a : ℂ) + J a)), and the pderiv operators act on real auxiliary coordinates by polynomial evaluation. Include both a=b and a≠b without nonzero assumptions on W, D_J, or N_0. Explicitly expose joint integrability for each final RHS and derive Gaussian-inner/Haar-outer form. These are undivided source insertions. The theorem needs no inverse matrix, deformation of Gaussian measure, complex conjugation of J, or Gaussian recentering. At J=0 the zeroth statement specializes to Eq10. Both source words retain distinct statements and source inputs.
