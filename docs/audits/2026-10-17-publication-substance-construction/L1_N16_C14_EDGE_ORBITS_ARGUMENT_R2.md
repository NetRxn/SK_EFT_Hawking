of the 16 four-bit site vectors preserves directions, the two anchored
plaquette words, and every signed coframe quarter increment. With periodic
boundary signs equal to +1 there is no wrapped-site phase. Transport each
Grassmann generator by its site, preserving its internal bar/psi and spin
label. The complete top monomial transforms by the determinant of a site
block permutation with eight generators per block. Swapping blocks has
sign (-1)^(8*8)=+1. Thus the scalar coefficient extracted by the original
Berezin order is preserved, even when individual ordered wedge products
change sign before reordering.

Translation also permutes the original based link anchors without changing
direction or chirality, so it preserves the product of independent normalized
sphere measures. The row factorial divisor is unchanged because the
multisets of local occupations and plane multiplicities are unchanged.
Consequently all sixteen translated configurations in an orbit contribute
the same number after signed contraction. This uses actual label transport
and does not identify opposite anchors, permute axes, or swap chirality.

The parity screen is a separate invariance. Simultaneously negate both
chiral quaternions at both original anchors of one unordered edge. The
coframe is homogeneous of degree one in the original pair at its own edge.
The plaquette trace is multilinear in the four actual anchored factors.

has zero integral. The exact roster check finds 48 even rows, forming
three translation orbits of size sixteen. Each representative is rooted
at the zero site, has two copies of each of a complementary plane pair,
and has the same S4 occupations and factorial divisor 512. All three
representative values still require actual exact contraction. Axis transport
would require an additional argument.
