SHA256 `4cd7033be2dd4265b228ff0a834d07715558b398b8fa16d8365392638fd4ea7e`.
Section 3, p. 7, Eq. (8) derives gate-sequence length exponent
log(5)/log(3/2), about 3.97. Equation (9) derives the separate
pointer-reuse runtime exponent log(3)/log(3/2), about 2.71, excluding
arithmetic precision costs. Section 5.1, p. 11, extends the bounds
to fixed dimension with dimension-dependent constants and lookup cost.

length exponent declarations. Independent final review accepted all ten

distinct from the published journal citation.
