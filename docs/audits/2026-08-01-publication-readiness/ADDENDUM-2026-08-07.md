## Correction to a claim made during the 2026-08-07 pass

⚠️ **The readiness formula was described as having no unreviewed-guard. It has one.**
`bundle_readiness.py:370-391` evaluates `not review_recorded → YELLOW (unreviewed)` before
reaching GREEN, and withholds GREEN when a P1 gate is blocked or could not be computed.

The actual defect is narrower and should be stated that way: **`review_recorded` does not
discriminate review KIND or SCOPE.** Any document named by `stage13_review_doc` satisfies it, so
a 16-anchor attribution sweep counts the same as a full adversarial pass. That is how D9 reached
GREEN with Stage 10 never run — not an absent guard, but a guard that cannot tell two very
different reviews apart.

## Correction — the chain-link figure

`chain_backing_targets_resolve` was built 2026-08-07 to gate what §5b of the remediation plan
identified as measured-but-ungated. It initially carried **its own resolver** and reported **156**
unresolvable links against §5b's **121** — a second resolver beside a working one, disagreeing
with it, which is the duplication §6a's approval sequence exists to prevent.

**Reconciled.** The local resolver is deleted; the check now calls
`chain_canonicalize.canonicalize_link` over `_iter_links` and reports **121 unresolvable
theorem/axiom/lemma links, 60 distinct targets, 25 papers** — matching `--report` exactly. An
AST test asserts no resolution helper can be reintroduced into the check.

§5b's caveat carries forward unchanged and is now recorded in the check itself: the count is an
**upper bound**, because ambiguous short names are counted `theorem-absent`.
