## Per-bundle findings

### D10 — *Kernel-Verified Foundations of Computational Quantum Chemistry and Open-System Dynamics*

**This manuscript is not publishable at any of its three named venues in its present form, and the
obstacle is not length.** Two of its three headline priority claims are attached to Lean content that
does not support them.

**What this manuscript currently is:** A well-written, clean-compiling 5-page synthesis that reads like
a competent extended abstract. It states three formalization "layers", references 31 live Lean theorem
names (I verified all 34 I checked resolve to real declarations), and carries a bibliography that
resolves perfectly. But it is a *table of contents for three subjects* rather than an article about any
of them: seven sections, each layer given ~500 words, no figures, no tables, no worked derivation, and
no equation that is developed rather than merely displayed. A referee at *J. Chem. Phys.* would find
nothing in it addressed to a computational chemist.

**Strongest asset:** `lean/SKEFTHawking/CoulombRelativeBound.lean` (2,492 lines) — the in-tree
Fourier–Kato development discharging the Coulomb relative bound `a < 1`, yielding a genuinely
**unconditional** `molecularHamiltonian_essSelfAdjoint (N m hm nuclei) : IsSelfAdjoint (…kineticOperator.closure + …potentialOperator)`
with no disclosed-hypothesis argument. I verified the signature and its proof term
(`obtain ⟨a,b,ha0,ha,hb,hrel⟩ := coulomb_isRelBounded m hm nuclei`). This is real, hard, and novel, and
it is currently one paragraph of a paper that claims two other things it has not done. **It should
probably be the whole paper.**

### D11 — *Kernel-Verified Topological Band Theory and Metamaterial Substrate*

**This is by a wide margin the better manuscript of the pair, and its scientific content is close to
honest. It is blocked by prose, not by physics.** The single sentence that best characterises the
problem is that D11 fixed its overclaim by *narrating the fix inside the manuscript*.

**What this manuscript currently is:** A genuinely well-argued 9-page paper with a real thesis — *"replace
the analytic construction with a discrete or algebraic certificate a kernel can check outright"* — that
is actually carried through five layers and closed by a substantive negative result. The abstract leads
with the result, the figures are real and captioned with theorem-level precision, and the deferrals are
stated rather than hidden. It is also, in at least six places, addressed to its own review process
rather than to a reader.

**Strongest asset:** §6 "Two refutations", specifically
`bernal_spectrum_not_determine_model` and the surrounding argument (`:483–498`). *"Formal verification
certifies that a proof is correct; it does not certify that the object proved about is the intended
one"* — backed by a four-way conjunction certifying that a wrong bilayer Hamiltonian was
spectrum-indistinguishable from the right one, caught only by an eigenvector-level invariant. This is
the most transferable result in the pair and would be cited outside the project. It is currently §6 of
a band-theory paper; it is arguably its own PRL.

## Cross-bundle observations

1. **The pair diverges sharply on every honesty axis, in the same direction.** D11 states the current
   toolchain pin; D10 states a superseded one. D11 states its search-coverage limits; D10 claims AFP
   coverage its own backing file denies. D11 verified and corrected its PhysLib attribution on
   2026-07-31; D10 carries the identical uncorrected error (D10-02). D11's `bundle_metadata.json` is
   internally consistent (RED, 23 blockers, 86 findings — all corroborated by `audit_log.jsonl`);
   D10's reports `stage13_status: green` / `blockers_open: 0` from a review predating the draft's own
   last modification. **The mechanical remedy is to run D11's 2026-07-31 correction pass over D10** —
   the two bundles were authorized the same day and D11 has already found the class of defects D10
   still carries.

2. **The scope-correction pattern cuts both ways, and this is the portfolio-level lesson.** D11 is the
   proof that the correction machinery works: I independently verified in the tree that
   `berryCurvature`/`berryConnection` do not exist, that no `H_BulkBoundaryLandmark` Prop exists, and
   that the manuscript promises neither. **But the correction landed as five separate passages of
   in-manuscript narration** (D11-01 through D11-05), each dated, each naming a review round. The
   remediation habit is currently "state the correction in the paper", and the correct habit is
   "correct the paper". Any bundle that has been through multiple Stage-13 rounds should be swept for
   the string patterns `corrected 20`, `earlier draft`, `round N`, `Stage-13`, `previously`.

3. **No content duplication between D10 and D11.** I checked the obvious seams — both touch spectral
   theory, both touch enclosure-style certificates, both make ITP-priority claims. Their Lean substrates
   are disjoint (D10: NEGF/DFT/Lindblad; D11: FHS/acoustic/non-Hermitian/effective-medium/graphene) and
   neither manuscript cites the other. The strategy asserts they are "siblings … the 2026-06-29 public
   substrate-breadth extension" (`PAPER_STRATEGY.md:172`, `:182`) and that D11 relates to D12 and D2,
   but **none of these sibling relationships appears as a citation in either manuscript.** For two
   papers submitted as a pair to overlapping venues, that is a gap a referee would notice.

4. **The methodological content is the strongest thing in the pair, and it is buried in both.**
   D11's `bernal_spectrum_not_determine_model` result ("verification certifies the proof, not the
   identification") and D10's two-layer honesty discipline are the same idea. It is currently §6 of a
   band-theory paper and a paragraph of a chemistry paper. The portfolio synthesis should consider
   whether this belongs in `I1` or as its own short paper — it is more likely to be cited than either
   host manuscript.

5. **Priority-claim policy should be set portfolio-wide from D11's template**, which is the best
   instance I have seen in this corpus: past-tense negative with a self-refutation guard, explicit
   list of which claimed-absent items the paper itself delivers, named carve-outs, pinned revision
   with the verification *method* stated, and bounded coverage limits. D10 makes five "to our
   knowledge … first" claims with none of that scaffolding, and at least one of them (DFT) is not
   defensible at all.
