### E1 — *A Falsifiable Hawking Spectrum for Polariton Microcavities at the Paris-LKB Device Parameters*

**Not sendable to the LKB team in its present form** — the physics ask is anchored to a cavity they do not have,
and the letter never says so.

**What this manuscript currently is:** A well-organised 5-page letter with two figures, a clear ask ("supply
`Γ_pol` and `τ_cav`"), and a boxed falsifiable window. Judged as Falque/Bramati/Giacobino would read it, it
is undermined by one thing: its Tier-1 perturbative-validity claim silently uses a 100 ps projected cavity
while their published device is 8 ps, and by the project's own classifier the 8 ps device is `intractable`.
Two long paragraphs of Phase-6q/6w substrate material sit between the device parameters and the physics,
addressed to no polariton experimentalist.

**Strongest asset:** the stimulated-Hawking detection argument. The universal threshold
`ω/κ < ln3/2π ≈ 0.175` is a genuinely clean, κ-independent, falsifiable statement, the `10³–10⁶` shot-count
advantage over spontaneous-correlation detection is exactly the kind of claim that changes an
experimentalist's plan, and §7's ask is concrete and reciprocal. That core is worth keeping intact.

### E2 — *Detecting Analog Hawking Radiation in the Bilayer-Graphene Dirac Fluid*

**Not sendable in its present form for one paragraph's sake** — but that paragraph is disqualifying, and
there is a second, deeper problem in the dissipation estimate.

**What this manuscript currently is:** The most complete of the three. 5 pp, four figures that are all
load-bearing (T_H vs throat length, dissipation window, noise PSD vs Johnson-Nyquist, per-bin SNR), a
closed-form observable, a boxed falsifiable window `ω/ω_H ∈ [0.5, 4]`, and an honest cross-device caveat
about adopting monolayer transport coefficients for a bilayer device. Then §6 detonates: a nested
retraction-of-a-retraction, dated, citing this project's internal audit round numbers and Lean source line
numbers, printed as body text.

**Strongest asset:** the observable. `ΔS_I(ω) = 2ℏω σ_Q Γ(ω) n_H(ω)` with a numeric peak
(`~10⁻²⁶ A²/Hz` at ~51 GHz), an explicit comparison to the `1.3×10⁻²⁴ A²/Hz` Johnson-Nyquist background, an
integration-time claim (~1 min), and a named dominant systematic (`Γ(ω)`) is exactly the shape of ask a
noise-spectroscopy group can act on. The quasi-1D safety margin (lowest transverse channel above `4ω_H`,
`ω_⊥(0) ≈ 1.38×10¹² s⁻¹`) is a real and checkable parametric guarantee.
