## Per-bundle findings

### D5

**This manuscript is not publishable and is not close.** It is a 14-page internal consolidation
memo wearing a PRD title.

**What this manuscript currently is:** Five sections whose *titles literally name the internal
source drafts they were pasted from* — "Dark-sector connections (paper~17)", "BBN classification of
Phase~5x DM candidates (paper~29)", "Strong-CP and topological dark energy (paper~32)",
"Equivalence-principle classification (paper~34)", "Cosmological-constant constraint (paper~42b
excerpt)" — followed by five sections of Phase 6m verdict reporting written in the project's own
review vocabulary (R5, CLEARED-R5, Track A/B/C, Tier I/II/III, Pipeline Invariant #14, MCC, lift,
bundle, wave). Every one of the seven figure captions ends with "Lifted from paperNN." The abstract
runs ~740 words and opens by telling the reader how many internal drafts were consolidated. A
referee at PRD reads two pages and sees a repository status report, not an article. The physics
underneath is real and in several places genuinely interesting; almost none of it is currently
*written* for a reader of the literature.

**Strongest asset:** §9 (Track B). The eight-mechanism entropic-gravity review is the one part of
the manuscript with a defensible external contribution: a mechanism-by-mechanism ledger against
DESI DR2 with honestly *differentiated* evidence strengths (2 Bayes-decisive, 1 σ-level with the
profile-systematics caveat stated, 1 AIC-moderate, 4 structural), each pinned to a named primary
source, with the mixed-unit aggregation explicitly refused. The 2026-06-10 D5-EV1 correction that
downgraded the cohort from 3-of-4 to 2-of-4 Bayes-decisive, and restated Verlinde as
σ-significance rather than a Bayes factor, is exactly the kind of self-correction that earns
referee trust. This section, expanded and re-voiced, is a publishable paper on its own.

## Answers to the bundle-specific questions

**1. Is D5 blocked?** No. Phase 6m Tracks A/B/C all returned (closure 2026-04-30, absorbed
2026-05-12); `RESEARCH_STATUS_OVERVIEW.md:339–355` and `ARCHITECTURE_SCOPE.md:162–190` record the
verdicts. `PAPER_STRATEGY.md` §2.2/§5/§6 are stale on this point. But the *framing* contingency the
strategy attached to that closure — Track C returned PARTIAL-VIABLE **and** a CLEARED-R5 family
preferred over ΛCDM — was never applied to the manuscript (D5-19). The draft is written against
neither the closed-scope framing nor the partial framing; it reports the partial outcome inside a
NO-GO-framed paper and never resolves the tension. That is a structural problem, not a copy-edit.

**2. NO-GO architecture at the strength the evidence supports.** Four of the five charter NO-GOs
are simply absent (D5-14). Of what remains: the $r_d$-independence claim is stated as a
recomputation but backed by a Bool-flag `rfl` (D5-13, P0-adjacent, filed P1 only because §9.1
discloses the sibling aggregators correctly); the Padmanabhan/CosMIn structural no-go *is*
disclosed at the right strength in the prose, matching its `KERNEL_NOGO_REGISTRY` entry — credit
where due; `entropic_gravity_no_go_count_eight` was correctly reframed from "establishes" to
"records the tally". For the absent four, I checked the Lean pre-emptively: the GD locking theorem's
"any framework" quantifier ranges over a project record whose isotropy is definitional (D5-15), and
the q-theory "all four KV constructions" claim is one ansatz plus a constant-valued enum map
(D5-16) — **not** three constructions plus an argument. Neither may be lifted at charter strength.
Nothing currently *in* the manuscript is a no-go presented above its kernel evidence except D5-13.

**3. Dark-matter forecast.** Unsourced at the point of use (D5-11, P0) and unreproducible as
stated (D5-12, P1). The draft's own numbers (0.83σ / 1.04σ single-cluster) are *more* conservative
than the charter's advertised 3.5–5.7σ — the draft is right and the charter is stale.

**4. Zhitnitsky ×240.** §5.1:454–458 reads better than expected: it gives the prediction
($6.7\times10^{-9}$ eV⁴), the ratio to observation (~240×), the honest framing *"well within the
three-orders-of-magnitude window quoted in the Zhitnitsky proposal"*, and identifies the actual
payoff as the 120-order suppression relative to $M_P^4$ — which is the defensible claim. Two
problems: the section has **zero citations** (`VanWaerbeke2025` sits uncited), and the figure
caption at 525–526 undercuts the text by describing the observed value as *"sitting close to the
prediction (within $\sim 2$ orders without free parameters)"* — 2 orders in the caption vs ~240× in
the text. Fix the caption to match, and cite the source.

**5. Genre coherence.** It is neither. It has a review's structure (survey of 21 literature
mechanisms organised into a taxonomy — *Physics Reports*) with an article's length (14 pp) and an
article's abstract conventions, and it lacks a review's completeness (no comparison tables, no
pedagogical development of GD, no coverage of the mechanism families it excludes from scope). My
recommendation: commit to **PRD long article** built around §9 + §10 + a properly-stated §12, with
the five legacy sections compressed to a short "substrate context" section — or split, sending the
DM half (§2.4, §4) to a separate paper where the forecast can be developed to referee standard.

**6. Figures.** Six of seven are canonically generated (D5-24); all seven are legacy lift artifacts.
They carry argument in the legacy sections — the EP-violation matrix and the BBN conformance matrix
are genuinely load-bearing, and the SFDM step-function money plot is the best figure in the paper —
but **the paper's claimed novel contribution (§8–§12) is entirely figure-free and table-free**
(D5-18). That imbalance is itself the finding: the decorative/argumentative split falls exactly
along the old-content/new-content line.
