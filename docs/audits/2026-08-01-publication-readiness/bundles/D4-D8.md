### D4

**D4 is not publishable as written, and the blocking problems are not length.** What this
manuscript currently is: a 14-page internal engineering register that has been given a
title. It addresses itself repeatedly to the project rather than to a reader — nine
subsections are literally headed or opened "Program-internal status" / "Program-internal
formalisation register" / "What this paper is not" — and its two most heavily promoted
results ("the substrate's central QEC theorem", "the substrate's knife-edge") are, in the
Lean, restatements of `Real.log_pos_iff` and `log x = 0 ↔ x = 1`. Meanwhile its flagship
knot invariants are discharged by `native_decide`, i.e. *not* kernel-checked, in a
manuscript whose abstract advertises formal-verification rigour and whose sibling D8
devotes a whole subsection (§sec:purity) to having eliminated exactly that trust escape.
A referee who opens the Lean will find both of these in under ten minutes.

**Strongest asset:** §4 (Drinfeld centre) and §3.1–3.2. The `S3CenterAnyons.lean`
content — the 8-anyon spectrum of `D(S_3)`, quantum dimensions `1,1,2,3,3,3,3,2`,
`D² = 36 = |S_3|²`, class equation — is concrete, checkable, non-vacuous, and genuinely
useful as a formalization datum. The six hexagon + four ribbon coherence equations are
also real content *if* re-discharged kernel-side. This is the nucleus of a legitimate
(shorter, mathematically-framed) paper.

### D8

**D8 is a well-written, honest, referee-credible short paper that is about one quarter of
the article its charter describes.** What this manuscript currently is: a dense, competent
9-page index of a large verified corpus — every section names its theorems, the scope
boundary is stated plainly and repeatedly, the novelty claims are hedged, the known gaps
(the Ross–Selinger prime-density wall, the non-sharp $\sqrt 2$, the Toffoli *upper* bound)
are shipped as limitations rather than buried. It reads like a paper. Its problem is that
it *announces* results instead of *presenting* them: there is not a single proof sketch,
not one worked derivation, no comparison table across the five alphabets, and the three
figures are sparse. A PRX Quantum referee would find nothing false and very little to
evaluate.

**Strongest asset:** the honest-scope discipline. `§sec:scope` (L715–744) and the abstract's
closing sentences state exactly what is and is not claimed; `§sec:rs` (L489–514) isolates
the number-theoretic wall to a named hypothesis, verifies that Hypothesis 8.3 of
Ross–Selinger equals Hypothesis 29 of Selinger 2015, and refuses to axiomatize it;
`§sec:su8` Route A says outright "in Route A, CCZ is *not* load-bearing". This is the
posture the whole portfolio should copy.

## Cross-bundle observations

**1. The re-scoping is half-executed, and the half that ran is the wrong half.** D8 received
the content (D8-02 confirms the substrate is real and the manuscript reports it), but D4
never gave it up (D4-07). The net state is duplication across two Tier-1 submissions to
adjacent venues: D4 §9.1–9.6 and D8 §2–§4 both present the alphabet-agnostic substrate,
the five-alphabet portfolio, the `log 5/log(3/2) ≈ 3.97` exponent, and a Mathlib-upstream
portfolio. If both are submitted as-is, a referee for either will find the other and ask
about self-plagiarism. **This must be fixed by deleting from D4, not by trimming D8** —
D8 is the designated home and is already too short. Fixing it makes D4 ~10 journal pages.

**2. The two papers apply contradictory verification standards to the same reader.** D8
§sec:purity (L614–637) explains at length that `native_decide` "extends the trusted base
from the kernel to the Lean compiler and host toolchain", reports eliminating four such
sweeps by structural reproof, and notes that "compiled evaluation is not accepted upstream"
(L712–713). D4 discharges its flagship trefoil invariant, its figure-eight invariant, all
six hexagon equations and all four ribbon equations by `native_decide` (D4-03) while its
abstract advertises "zero project-local axioms". Read side by side — and companion papers
*are* read side by side — this is the pair's single most damaging joint defect. It is worse
than either paper's individual problems because it establishes that the authors know the
standard and did not apply it uniformly.

**3. Neither manuscript actually cites the other.** D4 refers to "bundle D8" and to
`docs/PAPER_STRATEGY.md` (D4-06); D8 refers to "a companion bundle" and "a sibling bundle"
(D8-04). The relationship the strategy document treats as load-bearing exists in no
bibliography. The pair must be posted to arXiv together so each can cite the other by
identifier.

**4. Charter/manuscript divergence runs in opposite directions.** D4's charter promises a
chain-of-firsts and "the longest verified mathematical chain in any proof assistant"; the
manuscript contains none of it and instead repeatedly describes itself as
"program-internal" (D4-10, D4-11). D8's charter is faithfully reflected in the manuscript
but at one quarter the promised length (D8-01). The strategy document is stale with respect
to both — including the trefoil value (charter: "trefoil = −1"; manuscript and Lean:
$\langle\mathrm{trefoil}\rangle = -\sqrt2$, with $-1$ the normalized value). `PAPER_STRATEGY.md`
§2.2 should not be treated as describing these drafts.

**5. Remediation ordering.** D4-07 (delete §9 from D4) must run *before* any D8 expansion,
or the expanded D8 will duplicate live D4 text. D4-01/02/03 are independent of D8 and are
the true blockers on D4.

**6. Total remediation size class.** D8: `substantial-new-writing` (~4× expansion of an
existing, honest, verified core — no new mathematics required). D4: `restructure` +
`new-research-required` — the prose problems are a few days, but D4-01/02 cannot be
edited away, and reaching 40 pp needs Lean that does not exist. The realistic D4 outcome
is a ~15 pp mathematical-physics paper, not a 40 pp PRD article.
