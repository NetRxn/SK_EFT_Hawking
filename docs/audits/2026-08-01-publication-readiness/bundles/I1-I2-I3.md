## Per-bundle findings

### I1 — "Continuous Verification for Computational Physics Research"

**What this manuscript currently is:** a competent, well-organized 23-page methodology paper whose first ten pages are genuinely good — the three-layer framing is crisp, and Worked Cases 1 and 2 are told at the level of detail a skeptical physicist could actually evaluate (the counterexample coefficient vector, the saturation coupling, the run IDs). It then loses discipline: five pages (§11–§13, ~25% of the body) are an off-charter catalogue of classical-GR theorem counts that the paper itself says belong in a different paper; the third worked case — the one carrying one of the four closing "lessons" — rests on an Aristotle-batch claim with no entry in the project's own Aristotle registry; and the prose repeatedly stops explaining the methodology and starts defending a previous draft of itself. The program-scale facts (seventeen bundles, D1–D8, v4.29.0 toolchain, thirty-three checks) are 2–6 months stale.

**Strongest asset:** Worked Case 1 (§3, L331–452). It is fully told: the nine coefficients, the four-of-nine partition, the literal counterexample vector `c = (0,0,0,0,0,0,0,1,0)`, the seven algebraic relations that replaced the weak constraint, the run ID `270e77a0` (verified present in `ARISTOTLE_THEOREMS` at `constants.py:989`), and the downstream physics consequence (a paper "would have shipped the wrong noise floor"). This is the paper's whole argument working. Worked Case 2 is nearly as good and traces exactly to the block comment at `TetradGapEquation.lean:310–325`.

**Note on the Axis-2 exception.** I applied it. Describing Stage 13, the fourteen stages, the invariants, and the strengthening discipline is I1's legitimate subject matter and I have not flagged any of it as scar tissue. What I flag below is narrower: sentences where the manuscript defends *this draft* against *its own prior review*, and internal identifiers used at a reader who cannot resolve them.

### I3 — "Verified Stochastic Calculus and Large-Deviation Foundations for Lean 4 / Mathlib4"

**This bundle is not publishable at any venue, and the reason is that the artifact does not contain the mathematics its declarations are named for. This is the most serious finding in the tier.**

Taken together, those disclaimers subtract essentially all of the paper's content. What remains is 15 `Prop`-typed predicates whose bodies are continuity-and-vanishing-at-zero shape conditions, named after theorems of stochastic analysis that they do not state.

**Strongest asset:** §1's "Relation to existing libraries" paragraph (`:247-278`). That is exactly the hedging discipline the rubric asks for, and it should be held up as the model for the other bundles.

## Cross-bundle observations

1. **The `bundle_metadata.json` status fields are self-contradictory in all three bundles** and cannot be used as evidence by the synthesis. Every one carries `"stage13_status": "green"` alongside `blockers_open` of **16 (I1), 19 (I2), 16 (I3)** and `"readiness": "RED"`. This is the same defect the rubric already recorded for D7. 2. **The strategy document overclaims where the manuscripts do not.** `PAPER_STRATEGY.md` §2.4 asserts I3 delivers *"First formalization in any proof assistant of (i) the stochastic integral against semi-martingales… (iii) Itô's lemma for vector semimartingales; (iv) Novikov's condition."* The I3 draft explicitly withdraws all of these in §8.9. The charter is the document carrying the unhedged priority claim, and it feeds the flagship (F) and any grant or cover letter drawn from it. **Fix the charter, not just the draft** — otherwise the refuted claim re-enters at deposit time through a different door. Same pattern for I2 (charter's "114-theorem library" vs the draft's 949 declarations).

3. **I1 preaches the discipline I3 violates.** I1 §7 enumerates P4 ("self-discharging axiom… vacuous") and P5 ("falsifier as restated hypothesis… structural-tautology repackaging") as the project's named anti-patterns, and I1 §7's closing line is *"The strengthening discipline ensures that what we prove is what we claim."* I3's twelve modules are a textbook instance of exactly those patterns at library scale. If both papers ship as-is, a referee who reads them together has a free demolition. Conversely, **applying I1's own five-question checklist to I3's predicates is the concrete remediation path for I3-01** — the discipline already exists in-house; it was not applied here.
