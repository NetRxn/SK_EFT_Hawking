# D9 — referee-granularity assessment

**Bundle.** `papers/D9` — *Kernel-Verified Quantum-Network and Device-Characterization
Certification Substrate*. Target: PRX Quantum | Quantum. 1,318 lines of LaTeX.

**Readiness at audit time:** GREEN, 0 blockers, 0 advisories. See
[`PROMOTION-PATH-AND-SIGNAL.md`](../PROMOTION-PATH-AND-SIGNAL.md) for why that verdict exists.

**Assessment posture.** Read as a referee for the stated venue. The paper has real strengths and
they matter to the disposition: the two-layer posture (formula layer machine-verified /
identification layer literature-cited, never conflated) is a genuinely good methodological
contribution; the *Documented walls* section is unusually honest; and the formalized content
includes Watrous SDP duality with attainment of both optima, which is substantial work. The
blockers below are almost all avoidable, which is what makes them damaging.

---

## B1 — Toolchain pin is wrong (DISQUALIFYING for this paper)

**Where.** Appendix *Verification status*: *"The toolchain is pinned (Lean `v4.29.1`, Mathlib
`5e932f97`)"*.

**Actual.** `lean/lean-toolchain` = `leanprover/lean4:v4.32.0`; `lean/lakefile.toml` Mathlib
`rev="81a5d257c8e410db227a6665ed08f64fea08e997"`.

**Why disqualifying rather than an erratum.** The paper's sole claim is reproducible
kernel-checking. The first action of a competent referee is to clone the public repository and
build at the stated pin. It will not reproduce, and the failure presents as *the substrate does
not build*. This is the one claim the paper makes and the one thing a reader cannot do.

**Related.** The same appendix states that PhysLib-touching theorems were axiom-audited not to
depend on the single sorried declaration "present in that library at our pin." That audit was
performed at the **old** pin and is not carried forward.

## B2 — Substrate counts stale, and hardcoded

**Where.** Three sites (abstract, §Introduction, appendix): *"103 Lean modules and over 900
kernel-checked theorems,"* each hedged *"at the time of writing."*

**Actual**, measured from `lean_deps.json`: **104 modules, 1,123 theorem/lemma declarations** in
the `SKEFTHawking.QuantumNetwork` family.

**Assessment.** Underclaims by roughly 20%, so it is not an integrity issue in the way B1 and B3
are. It is a *credibility* issue: hand-maintained literals sit beside a repository that generates
these figures (`counts.tex` macros, used elsewhere in the same document for project-wide totals).
The triple "at the time of writing" reads as an author who knew the numbers would rot.

## B3 — The Methods disclosure asserts a process that did not occur

**Where.** §Methods and tools disclosure, verbatim:

> *Manuscript prose was AI-assisted and human-reviewed; all numerical claims trace to the
> repository's computation pipeline through its automated validation checks.*

**Actual.** D9 has **no `claims_review.json`**, a **0-byte `audit_log.jsonl`**,
`stage10_status: pending`, and `last_stage10_review: None`. The sentence-level claims review —
the mechanism that produces the trace being claimed — has never run on this bundle.

**Why this is the most serious finding.** It is a false statement about verification process, in
the disclosure section, of a paper whose subject is verification integrity. The paper invites
readers into the public repository, where those fields are directly visible in
`bundle_metadata.json`. It compounds with the (correct, and correctly disclosed) statement that
proof drafting used LLM agents: an AI-assisted formalization paper carrying an unbacked
"human-reviewed" claim is the specific artifact the field is currently primed to distrust.

**This sentence should not appear in any bundle until the process it describes has actually run.**

## B4 — No demonstrated use

The motivation is simulator disagreement (Chung 2025, cross-verification of quantum network
simulators). The envelope is never run against those simulators, and no case is shown where a
published or simulated fidelity falls outside a proven interval.

For the stated venue this is the difference between a library announcement and a result. **One
demonstrated catch** would change the disposition of the paper more than any other single
addition.

## B5 — Conditionals without a discharged example

The two-layer posture makes every physically meaningful statement an implication whose antecedent
is an unverified modeling hypothesis. This is the honest construction and the paper defends it
well. A hostile referee inverts it: *nothing about any device has been certified; a large family
of implications has been proven.*

The answer is one worked end-to-end example where the hypotheses are discharged from real
datasheet values — $T_1$, transmissivity, readout window in; certified interval out. The paper
contains none.

## B6 — Unsupported priority claim

*"the swap-chain envelope as (to our knowledge) the first formally verified protocol-level network
fidelity bound."* Referees are hostile to unsupported firsts. Either support it with a stated
search, or weaken to "we are not aware of prior formalization."

## B7 — Abstract is unreadable

A single sentence spans layers (i)–(v), roughly 250 words before the first full stop. Most readers
will not finish it. See [`PROSE-QUALITY-BASELINE.md`](../PROSE-QUALITY-BASELINE.md) — D9 is the
worst-scoring bundle in the corpus on every readability axis measured.

## B8 — Verification status is an appendix

For this paper the verification posture *is* the result. It is currently material a reader reaches
after the bibliography.

---

## Disposition

**Not submittable.** B1 and B3 are integrity-class and must be fixed before the draft is shown to
anyone outside the project. B2 is mechanical. B4 and B5 are the difference between acceptance and
rejection at the stated venue and require new work, not editing.

**The reputational exposure is B1 + B3 together**, and it generalizes beyond this paper: the
inference available to a referee is not *this paper has errors* but *this author claims
machine-checked rigor and cannot state their own toolchain version correctly* — an inference that
attaches to the author and transfers to the other twenty bundles, which make the same kernel-purity
claims from the same substrate.

**Pin-drift is a corpus-wide class, measured**, not a D9 defect. Scanning every
`paper_draft.tex` for a cited Lean version against the live `4.32.0`:

| | drafts citing a stale Lean version |
|---|---|
| publication bundles | **8 of 21** — D6, D9, D10, L2 (`4.29.1`); E1, E2, I3 (`4.29.0`); I1 (`4.28.0` *and* `4.29.0`) |
| legacy per-paper drafts | 8 |
| **total** | **16** |

B1 must be swept, not fixed in place. ⚠️ Scan on the **Lean version string only** — a scan for
Mathlib revs by hex pattern produces false positives from ISBNs, Aristotle run IDs and dates, and
will manufacture findings.
