### D2

**This manuscript is not publishable in its current form, and the gap is not editorial.**

**What this manuscript currently is:** An 11-page annotated index of a Lean library, organised around four
physics threads it does not derive. It contains 191 `\texttt{}`/`\verb||` Lean identifiers across ~6,600
body words — one module-or-theorem name every 35 words — and its longest subsection (§2.4, 939 w) is a
recitation of nine Lean filenames with essentially no mathematical exposition. Its 29 headings average
~230 words each; three section-level headings are 34–96-word openers. A referee at PRD would find that
the central modular derivation is printed as a tautology (see D2-03), that a section titled *"Discharging
hypothesis H2"* cites a Lean theorem whose entire content is `rank = rank` (D2-01), and that the paper's
own repository records H2 as **OPEN**. The paired 4-page letter L2 is, on its shared material, the more
complete and more careful document.

**Strongest asset:** §2.4's Rokhlin decomposition is genuinely substantial mathematics — the
`SmoothSpinManifold4` interface isolating `even_unimod` + `topo : 2 | σ/8` from the unconditional
algebraic floor `8 | latticeSig`, with the Hasse–Minkowski and theta-modularity inputs discharged and the
lattice-Arf bridge machine-refuted. Presented as *mathematics* rather than as a filename tour, that is a
real, checkable, publishable contribution — arguably the best single result in the pair.
