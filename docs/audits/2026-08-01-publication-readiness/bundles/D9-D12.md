## Per-bundle findings

### D9

**This manuscript is not publishable as a Tier-1 article in its present form.**

**What this manuscript currently is:** a well-written, honest, carefully-scoped *annotated index*
of a Lean corpus — 192 `\lean{}` theorem names threaded through 12 pages of connective prose,
organised into five layers. The prose quality is genuinely high and the two-layer posture is
stated with unusual discipline. But it is an index, not an article: there are **zero numbered
equations** in the entire manuscript, **zero tables**, and no single result is developed far
enough for a reader to understand *why* it is true or what it costs. A PRX Quantum referee would
recognise a serious body of work and then ask where the paper is.

**Strongest asset:** the two-layer posture as stated in §1 (L122–155) and its worked instance on
PLOB (L143–150) — "the corpus formalizes the *rate-bound function* … and states plainly that the
channel-converse semantics is the cited theorem of Ref. [PLOB2017], not a formalized one." That
paragraph is the paper's real contribution and it is well-argued. Likewise the "Documented walls"
subsection (§6.3, L978–1012), which is exactly the honest-negative-space section this kind of
paper should have and which most such papers omit.

**1. The stack claim is true in the Lean and absent from the manuscripts.** The strategy's

by direct read of `HelstromDiscrimination.lean` (both `IsBinaryPOVM` and

D6, I3 (D9-08). A reader of either paper cannot follow the stack. Both fixes are trivial and both
should land before anything else in this pair.

**2. The boundary itself holds — this is the good news.** D12 does *not* re-derive D9's envelopes.
`paper_draft.tex:577–579` is explicit: `relaxation_ceiling` and `relaxation_thermal_ceiling` are
"format anchors rather than … new results: each is a one-line re-casting of a pre-existing
quantum-network floor into fidelity form, and we do not count them among the three." That is
exactly the right posture and exactly the right disclosure. The defect is purely citational: the
pre-existing floor is never named or attributed. No duplicated *content* was found between the two
bundles.

   goes further and explains that PhysLib enters only through the import closure). **Correct.**
